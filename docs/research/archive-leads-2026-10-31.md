## Totals

- New programs: 12 (10 with checked details, 2 listed with details pending).
- Pending programs finished: 2 (Texas Tech HSC, SUNY Upstate).
- Leads that were already listed or covered by an existing umbrella: Tulane, UCSD Biology, Louisville IPIBS, UC Irvine CMB, UCSF, plus parts of Weill Cornell, Wake Forest and the SDSU joint program. No duplicates were added.
- Not confirmed: Nebraska's BRTP (nothing added).

## Leads found along the way, not yet added

Molecular Metabolism and Nutrition, Evolutionary Biology, Ecology and Evolution, Development,
Regeneration and Stem Cell Biology, Biochemistry and Molecular Biophysics, Human Genetics and

Forest's Biomedical Engineering and Medical Physics PhDs apply separately. Each needs its own check.
