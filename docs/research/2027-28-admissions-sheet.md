The community sheet for the season our site serves: people applying now
(autumn 2026), hearing back in early 2027 and starting in fall 2027. Junaid
asked for it to be read in detail; Claude read it on 2026-10-18, about two
days after it was posted. Read `README.md` in this folder first for the
rules on using community sheets.

  (https://discord.gg/5u7qQS6y9A). It was built from last season's main
  sheet: the welcome text and the example row still carry last season's
  thread name and dates.
- **State on 18 October:** temporarily view-only while the organisers fix
  edits people made, and **no results yet**. Across all 43 tabs there are
  only four stray cells: a comment left over from last season, one ticked
  box, and two junk entries.
- **Program list taken from it:** `2027-28-sheet-programs.csv` (580 rows,
  described at the end).
- **What was read:** cell by cell, the four information tabs and the
  Biosciences, Chemistry, Engineering, Health Sciences, Health Policy and
  Human Development tabs. The other fields' tabs were scanned for results

## Columns

Every field tab follows the template. Biosciences and a few others add a
Track column (Business calls it Concentration). The Biosciences layout:

University/Institute · Program · Track · Degree · four tick boxes
(Interviews out? Acceptances out? Waitlists out? Rejections out?) · Date of
interview invitation · Interview dates · Interview method · Domestic or
international (for interviews) · Acceptance date · Domestic or
international (for acceptances) · Waitlist date · Rejection date · Notes.

The Degree cell comes filled in as "Doctoral (Ph.D., DPhil)"; other values
used are "Master's (M.S.)", "Master's (M.A., M.S.)", "Master's (M.A., M.S.,
M.Eng)" and, in arts tabs, "Terminal Other (e.g. MFA)".

Compared with the Fall 2024 sheet, this layout adds waitlists and tracks,
and drops the deadline, "how and from whom", head counts, last year's
dates and rolling admissions.

## House rules (in our words)

The welcome tab sets these rules. Several are worth adopting as they are:

1. **Only formal interviews count:** invitations from the admissions
   committee that are part of the application process. A professor's
   informal video call before the interview season isn't an interview
   invite; it goes in the notes.
2. **Say which stage it is** when a program interviews in steps (for
   example, a short screening call by invitation in December, then
   interviews in January).
3. **Domestic or international means compared with the institution's
   country.**
4. **Professors by initials only**, if at all, and nobody should be pushed
   to share them. No private details such as email addresses or full names.
5. **Research degrees only:** no law, medical or other professional
   degrees.
6. Housekeeping: add schools in alphabetical order with the abbreviation in
   brackets, merge the institution cells when adding a program under an
   existing school, don't filter or sort the shared sheet (make a copy),
   and keep one tab per field rather than per degree.
7. Be kind and respectful.

## What it means for us

1. **Same four outcomes as ours.** Our results already record interview,
   accepted, waitlisted and rejected, with domestic or international, so
   the main outcomes match.
2. **Two fields we don't collect yet:** interview format (in person,
   virtual, both) and the interview dates themselves (the sheet keeps the
   invite date and the interview dates apart). Worth adding as optional
   fields on interview reports.
3. **Borrow the definitions for our report form's help text:** only a
   formal invitation from the program counts as an interview invite; a
   professor's informal call doesn't. Domestic or international is
   compared with the program's country.
4. **Tracks matter.** The sheet needed a Track column, as our catalog will.
5. **The Q&A tab is a discussion thread in disguise.** People want to ask
   "has anyone heard from X?" per program; that's our planned discussion
   threads, kept apart from results.
6. **Fragility again.** About two days after it was posted, the sheet was
   view-only while edits were repaired. Last season's main sheet was locked
   in February and later deleted (see `README.md`).
7. **Look again later.** The sheet fills from about December. Re-reading it
   in mid-December and again in February would show this season's timing,
   using the patterns only.

## About `2027-28-sheet-programs.csv`

Columns: `institution`, `program`, `track`, `degree`, `tab`, `sheet_row`.

- **Biosciences:** all 402 rows that name a program or a track.
- **Chemistry, Pharmacology & Toxicology:** the 104 rows whose program or
  track mentions pharmacology or pharmaceutics, toxicology, medicinal
  chemistry, drugs, or "bio" (chemical biology, biochemistry, biophysics).
- **Engineering:** the 74 rows for biomedical engineering, bioengineering,
  and biological, medical or biotechnology engineering. Chemical and
  biomolecular engineering is left out, and so is the one matching row
  with no institution.

Institution names are filled in from merged cells and keep the sheet's
spelling, typos and bracketed notes included. `degree` is `PhD` for
"Doctoral (Ph.D., DPhil)", `Masters` for any master's value, and blank
when the cell was empty or held something else. `sheet_row` is the row
number in that tab, for finding the row in the sheet. GitHub shows the
file as a searchable table.
