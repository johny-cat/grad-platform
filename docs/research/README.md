# Research notes

What applicants track, ask and worry about, learned from the community
spreadsheets they share each season. These notes shape what we build.
They are never a source of program facts.

## Rules for using these notes

1. **Leads, not facts.** Program names, deadlines and dates in community
   sheets are typed by applicants and are often wrong or out of date.
   Before a program goes into `src/data/programs/`, every fact is checked
   on its official page (golden rule 1 in `CLAUDE.md`).
2. **Don't republish people's posts.** Comments are other people's words,
   and some name professors or applicants. Use the patterns, not the posts.
3. **Their results aren't ours.** The site shows only its own users'
   results (see `docs/DECISIONS.md`), so results are never imported from
   these sheets. Their counts are their users, not totals.

## The community sheets, season by season

Each season applicants run one big Google Sheet with a tab per field, and
some fields have their own sheet too. The main sheets are named after the
academic year students start: "2026-2027" means people applied in late
2025, heard back in early 2026 and started in fall 2026.
