# BME 2026–27 admissions sheet (last season)

Biomedical engineering and bioengineering results from the season before
ours: people applied in late 2025, heard back between December 2025 and the
summer of 2026, and started in fall 2026. It is the only sheet close to our
field with last season's results that still opens: that season's general
sheet and its biosciences sheet are gone (see `README.md`, which also has
the rules for using community sheets). Junaid asked for it to be read in
detail; Claude read every cell of all five tabs on 2026-10-18.

- **Sheet:** "BME Graduate Admissions 2026-2027",
  https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1xBej2dHrGHE2PrJvoWYUO4MS_bV3ByYdm-xWCayHzcM/edit
- **How it started:** an applicant made it on 3 February 2026, when that
  season's main sheet was made view-only in the middle of the season. The
  rows up to that day were copied from the main sheet's Engineering tab;
  people kept adding to it into the summer. Requests went through a Reddit
  thread:
  https://www.reddit.com/r/gradadmissions/comments/1qv7mmc/bmebioe_graduate_admissions_spreadsheet/

## Tabs

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Welcome/Info! | Why the sheet exists, instructions (add schools alphabetically, merge the school's cells when adding a program, no private details: initials are enough) and rules (be kind, don't delete other people's entries) |
| BME | 104 rows at about 75 institutions: 100 PhD programs, 3 master's and one row typed into the wrong columns |
| Program Q&A | 33 questions asked between 3 February and 8 April 2026, nearly all "has anyone heard from…", with answers |
| Sheet8, Sheet9 | Empty |

Columns of the BME tab: University · Program · Degree type · three tick
boxes (Interviews out? Acceptances out? Rejections out?) · International or
domestic · Date of interview invitation · Interview dates · Interview
method · **Stipend details** · Acceptance date · Rejection date · Notes ·
**Chat**.

Two things this sheet added on its own: a stipend column, and a separate
Chat column so conversation stays out of the notes. It has no waitlist
column, so people wrote waitlists into the notes and the Q&A tab.

## What the numbers show

From the BME tab, using the first date anyone gave in each row. These are
the sheet's users, not totals.

- **Activity:** 94 of the 104 rows have at least one tick: interviews out
  71, acceptances out 59, rejections out 55. 65 rows have notes and 11 use
  the Chat column.
- **First interview invite** (53 rows with a date): 1–15 December 3;
  16–31 December 18; 1–15 January 15; 16–31 January 14; 1–15 February 2.
  One more is a professor's informal meeting in September. So invites ran
  from mid-December to the end of January. Fall 2024's biology programs
  were a little earlier, with about 7 in 10 first invites in December.
- **Interviews and visits:** virtual interviews mostly in December and
  January; in-person visit weekends mostly in the second half of February
  and early March.
- **First acceptance** (36 rows): late December 3; 1–15 January 5; 16–31
  January 8; 1–15 February 7; 16–28 February 5; 1–15 March 4; 16–31 March
  2; early April 1; and one in July, after a professor found funding.
- **First rejection** (21 rows): January 5; February 9; March 4; early
  April 3.
- **Interview format** (63 rows): virtual 34, in person 23, hybrid or other
  6. Many programs did a virtual round first and a campus visit later.
- **Who was notified** (69 rows): international 31, domestic 21, both 16,
  one row with mixed reports.
- **Stipends:** 24 rows mention one; the 19 yearly figures run from
  $30,000 to $52,000, with a median of $38,000. These are crowd reports:
  never use them as program facts. Official stipends go in a program's
  `funding.stipend`, from its own pages.

## What people asked and noticed

1. **"Was this from a professor or the program?"** Many invites came first
   from one professor (often in December), then from the program (in
   January). People kept asking whether an invite was formal or informal,
   and whether silence after a professor's call meant a rejection. Our
   report form should ask applicants to report only formal invitations, as
   this season's sheet now asks (see `2027-28-admissions-sheet.md`).
2. **Direct admission or rotations.** In many BME programs a professor
   admits you into their lab ("direct admit"); in others you rotate
   through labs. People often didn't know which kind they had applied to,
   and the process differs completely. Our catalog already records this as
   `admissionModel` (program, lab, project); program pages and results
   should make it visible.
3. **Visit weekends.** "Recruitment events", "open houses" and "visit
   weekends" were sometimes the interview and sometimes a celebration for
   people already admitted, and people couldn't tell which. One event was
   said to have invited only domestic applicants. Supports the "visit
   invite" step suggested in the Fall 2024 notes.
4. **Rolling decisions.** People compared the date they applied with the
   date others heard back, because some programs decide on a rolling
   basis, and direct-admit programs kept sending offers until April. Program pages should say "decisions on a rolling basis"
   when the official pages say so.
5. **Official live statistics.** Applicants used Georgia Tech's official
   doctoral program statistics page, which shows offers made so far this
   year, to estimate how many offers were still to come. Program pages
   could link official statistics like this where they exist (Duke
   publishes some too; see the Fall 2024 notes). Linked from the sheet,
   not checked yet:
   https://lite.gatech.edu/lite_script/dashboards/doctoral_program_stats.html
6. **Funding decides offers.** Offers hung on a professor's grant: one fell
   through when the grant did, one came in July once funding was found,
   one PhD admit was moved to a master's for lack of funding, and one
   reported stipend came with a warning that funding couldn't be
   guaranteed for the whole PhD because of cuts. Two people were turned
   down for the PhD but offered a master's; one found that only as a new
   application in the portal, with no email. Worth an option on reports
   ("offered a master's instead") and funding news on program pages.
7. **Waitlists late in the season.** Waitlist news came as late as early
   May. Our waitlisted outcome is needed, and late-season activity should
   stay visible.
8. **"Is this real?"** People doubted entries: a tick that might be a
   misclick, a rejection that looked fake, a deleted program name, an
   entry for a program said not to exist, interview posts on GradCafe that
   looked suspicious. Our design answers this (one report per person per
   decision, nobody edits other people's reports, flagging, "others
   reported this too"); keep it.
9. **Portal wording.** People tried to decode statuses such as "under
   review", "awaiting decision", "under program review" and "eligible for
   admission", and emails that only said to check the portal. A short
   explainer could help later.
10. **When to follow up.** People wanted to know how long to wait after a
    professor's interview before asking politely about their status. A
    topic for the Not Yet community and its guides.
11. **International applicants** asked constantly whether anyone
    international had heard, as in Fall 2024. Our domestic and
    international counts on every results summary answer this.
12. **What interviews are like.** People shared what they were asked
    (their research, why a PhD, why this school, a few technical
    follow-ups). That belongs in discussion threads, not in results.
13. **Fixed dates and April 15.** Some programs told applicants that every
    decision would be out by a fixed date, and people mentioned the
    15 April reply deadline. Our season timeline already marks 15 April.

## For our season timeline

`src/config.ts` today shows interviews from 5 January to 10 March and
offers from 1 February to 1 April. Both this sheet and the Fall 2024 sheet
point earlier: invites from early or mid-December to the end of January,
visits mostly in February and early March, and offers from mid-January
(some in late December) to April. Suggested phases: invites 1 December to
31 January, interviews and visits mid-December to mid-March, offers
mid-January to 15 April.
