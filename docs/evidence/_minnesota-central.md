### https://grad.umn.edu/admissions/application-instructions/application-fees/gsdo-application-fee-waiver

> [source line 29] Applicants requesting a fee waiver from the University of Minnesota’s Graduate School Diversity Office must meet the following criteria to receive consideration:

> [source line 30]   * Be a U.S. citizen or permanent resident

> [source line 31]   * Be seeking to obtain a graduate degree for the first time (current graduate students, those who are seeking a second graduate degree, and those who have already earned a terminal degree are not eligible)

> [source line 32]   * Meet at least ONE of the following:

> [source line 33]     * Participated in a graduate school preparation program: Must have participated in a program directed by a program leader that prepares students to earn an advanced degree and pursue an academic and/or research career.

> [source line 34]     * Submitted an inquiry at a UMN graduate school recruitment event: Must have visited the GSDO recruiter/booth at a recruitment conference/event and completed our GSDO inquiry form.

> [source line 35] ## Steps to obtain an application fee waiver:

> [source line 36]   1. Start an online application, but do not submit the application until you have received confirmation that the application fee has been waived.

> [source line 37]   2. Send the GSDO an email ([email protected]) with the subject Application Fee Waiver.

> [source line 38]     1. Attached to or included in the email must be proof of participation/membership in a program/organization (typically a letter from the program/organization director, documentation, verification form, etc.) or the recruitment event at which you completed a GSDO inquiry form.

> [source line 39]   3. The GSDO will send verification via email of the applied waiver or may request additional documentation/proof, if needed.

> [source line 40]   4. The applicant can submit their application without paying the fee once an email receipt has been sent stating that the application fee has been waived.

> [source line 30] Education

> [source line 32] #### Secondary School Education

> [source line 34] If you completed secondary school in the U.S. or in a country on  this list,* then you satisfy the basic English language proficiency requirements.

> [source line 36] #### Or

> [source line 37] #### Post-secondary Education

> [source line 39] You satisfy the English proficiency requirement if you completed post-secondary coursework in the U.S. or a country on this list,* and:

> [source line 41]   1. the coursework consisted of at least 16 semester credits, or 24 quarter credits, and

> [source line 42]   2. ESL coursework was not part of the qualifying number of credits, and

> [source line 43]   3. the coursework was completed within the past 2 years, and

> [source line 44]   4. the coursework was completed while living in the U.S. or in a country on this list*

> [source line 45] * Not all countries where English is an official language or is widely spoken are included on the English proficiency exemption list. The English Language Requirement is set by a University-wide ESL Policy committee which includes representatives from: Undergraduate Admissions Office, Graduate Admissions Office, Minnesota English Language Program (MELP), International Student and Scholar Services (ISSS), and the UMD Graduate School.
