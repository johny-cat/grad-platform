# Johns Hopkins central pages (School of Medicine graduate programs)
Checked 2026-10-20 by opening the pages in a headless browser (Chromium) and saving their full text; quotes are copied from that text. The PDF pages were downloaded by the same browser and read as text. The program evidence files refer to these pages by URL.

Used by the School of Medicine PhD programs checked in this batch: Cellular and Molecular Medicine, Human Genetics and Genomics, Neuroscience and XDBio (the BCMB PhD was checked earlier). The Biochemistry and Molecular Biology PhD belongs to the Bloomberg School of Public Health, a different school: its site did not open (see the section Pages that did not open), so it is not covered here.

- applications close at the deadline: https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/som/education-programs/graduate-programs/admissions — "Application materials may not be submitted after the deadline."
- application fee 125 USD, one fee for up to four School of Medicine PhD programs: https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/-/media/som/documents/_documents-grad-program/2025/application-fees-and-fee-waiver-request-instructions-2025.pdf (linked from the on-campus page as "Application fees and fee waivers"; the file name says 2025 but the text says 2026-2027) — "The 2026-2027 application fee is $125 per program." ; "Prospective students interested in the following PhD programs may apply to four of these PhD programs and only pay one application fee of $125:" ; the list includes "Cellular and Molecular Medicine", "Human Genetics", "Neuroscience" and "XDBio: Cross Disciplinary Graduate Program in Biomedical Sciences" ; "Application fees are non-refundable and must be paid by credit card."
- fee waivers: same PDF — "Application fee waivers are available to prospective students who meet one or more of the three eligibility criteria described below." ; "Applicants who are U.S. Citizens or Permanent Residents of the U.S. are eligible for a fee waiver if their income meets the financial eligibility criteria" ; "your annual household income may not exceed 200% of the current U.S. poverty guidelines listed." ; "Current or former members of the U.S. Armed Forces are eligible for fee waivers." ; "Any Johns Hopkins University or Kennedy Krieger Institute summer internship program or postbaccalaureate program" ; "Any undergraduate college or university located in the state of Maryland" ; "If you are not a U.S. Citizen or Permanent Resident and meet eligibility criterion #3 (alumni status), please email Gradadmissions@jhmi.edu with your supporting information to receive the waiver."
- English tests (same wording in the CMM, Human Genetics and Genomics, Neuroscience and XDBio PDFs; no minimum score is stated in any of them): "International students whose native language is not English are required to complete the Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL) or International English Language Testing System (IELTS)." ; "Scores must be received before the application deadline." ; "The TOEFL requirement will be waived if the applicant has completed their degree in a U.S. institution, or is currently enrolled and will receive a degree from a university within the U.S. prior to matriculation at JHU SOM." ; https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/som/education-programs/graduate-programs/admissions (FAQ) — "TOEFL requirements vary by program.
