> Requirements for Admission (MS, PhD)

> Admission criteria for the Department of Biological Sciences thesis-based MS and PhD programs include a minimum scholastic index (grade point average on a 4.0 scale) of 2.8 overall and 3.0 in the sciences. Applicants are required to have completed the following coursework, or its equivalent: two years of biological sciences; one year of mathematics, preferably including calculus and/or statistics; one year of college physics; one year of general chemistry; and one course in organic chemistry.

> Applicants who did not major in biology may still be considered if they have completed two years of biological sciences coursework and have a strong academic background in other scientific disciplines.

> Foreign National Applicants

> International applicants must earn a score of at least 100 on the Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL) or at least 7.5 on the International English Language Testing System (IELTS), in accordance with University of Delaware requirements for graduate assistantship eligibility. Previous education, training, or residence in the U.S. does not automatically exempt foreign nationals from these requirements.

> Requests to waive the language testing requirement - for example, for students from English-speaking countries outside the U.S. or for foreign students who earned a college degree from a U.S. institution - must be approved by the University of Delaware Graduate College.

> Admission to the graduate programs is competitive. Meeting the stated requirements does not guarantee admission, and applicants who do not meet every requirement may still be considered if they demonstrate other appropriate strengths.

> Requirements for Application (MS, PhD)

> Applications must include three letters of recommendation from individuals able to assess the applicant's potential for graduate study, an undergraduate transcript, a resume or CV describing academic and/or work experience, and a personal statement. The GRE is neither required nor considered. The personal statement should address the following questions:

>   1. What scientific research experience have you had?

>   2. What are your research interests and long-term professional objectives?

>   3. What specific attributes of our Department make you feel that it would be a good place for you?

>   4. If applicable, are there any special circumstances related to your academic record that you feel we should know about?

> Applications are considered only for fall entrance. For optimal consideration for Fall admission, all application materials should be submitted by December 15th, although applications will be considered until April 15th if there are remaining positions in the program. Foreign nationals are encouraged to apply by the full consideration deadlines to give sufficient time to obtain the necessary visas for graduate study in the USA.

>   3. What if I don’t know which laboratory in which to conduct my research?  PhD students are not admitted directly into research mentor laboratories. MS students must  chose their research mentor at the beginning of their first semester in the program. Ph.D. students perform 2-3 laboratory rotations during the first year in the program and choose their mentor from these rotation experiences.

>   4. When is the application deadline? Our priority deadline is December 15th of each academic year. Applications that come in after this deadline will be considered if additional space is available. Applications for international students will not be considered if received after April 1st and domestic applicants will not be considered if received after April 15th.

>   5. How large is the Biological Sciences graduate program? As of fall 2026, there are over 75students enrolled in our thesis-based MS & PhD graduate programs, all of which receive tuition scholarships and a stipend.

## https://www.udel.edu/academics/colleges/grad/prospective-students/programs/biological-sciences/

> The Department of Biological Sciences offers Master of Science and Doctor of Philosophy degrees with focus on cell and developmental biology, neurobiology, microbiology, physiology, molecular biology, and genetics. Formalized concentrations provide students with focused research training and individualized career mentorship. All research PhD students receive financial support through assistantships.

> PhD graduate assistants in Biological Sciences also receive a full tuition scholarship from the University of Delaware and university-subsidized health insurance.

> #### Program Concentrations:

>   * Cell and Organ Systems Physiology Concentration (M.S., Ph.D.)

>   * Chemistry-Biology Interface Concentration (Ph.D.)

>   * Molecular Biology and Genetics Concentration (M.S., Ph.D.)

> If you qualify

> If you qualify for one of the fee waiver categories listed above, check the appropriate box for the program on the application. After submitting the graduate application, you will then need to upload documentation from your program leadership confirming your participation and affiliation with the program on your application status page
