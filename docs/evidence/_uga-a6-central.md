Checked 2026-10-29.

https://grad.uga.edu/admissions/application-fee/
About and Application Fee Waivers: since July 2, 2024, first application 75 and additional applications 25; limited qualifying-participant waivers require uploaded proof. Exact anchor: “$75 for the first application”. Old departmental international fee 100 is superseded.

https://grad.uga.edu/admissions/requirements/international-applications/english-language-proficiency-requirement/
Minimum list: legacy TOEFL 80 with Speaking/Writing 20; new tests on/after January 22, 2026 require overall 4.5 and Speaking/Writing 4. No January 21 rule inferred. IELTS 6.5, each band 6; UGA Intensive English Level 6 alternative. MyBest/Home Edition and IELTS Online/One Skill Retake accepted, agency reporting required; DET not accepted. No exemption inferred from English-medium education.

Independent review 2026-10-29: reopened central English page, degree/study exemption paragraphs and TA note. Degrees from accredited US institutions or institutions in primary-English countries usually exempt. Current US study for at least one year with strong performance (example GPA 3.0) may support waiver. Department can still require testing; TA policy separate. These are conditional routes, not a blanket foreign English-medium exemption.
