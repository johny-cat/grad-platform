# Auburn central policy
Checked 2026-10-28

https://graduate.auburn.edu/admissions/apply.php
Application requirements: bachelor or equivalent; transcripts from all institutions (unofficial at application, official before enrollment). International records: original language plus certified English translation and degree certificates. Fee: international 70 USD, domestic 60 USD. Waivers: currently enrolled Auburn graduate students, full-time employees, active-duty military confirmation, second approved-dual-program application. Select category and supply proof. Central outer deadlines 45 days domestic and 90 international; departments may require earlier.
