# northwestern-ibis-phd

## Official details checked 2026-10-23

https://ibis.northwestern.edu/graduate/prospective/
> “Applications to the Interdisciplinary Biological Sciences (IBiS) program for Fall 2027 should be submitted online to The Graduate School by December 1, 2026 for consideration for full financial support (tuition, stipend, and health insurance).”
> “The application fee is $95.00.”
> “The IBiS program is unable to provide fee waivers directly”
> “IBiS does not require the submission of GRE scores”
> “Academic Statement (500-1,000 words)”; “Personal Statement (125-250 words)”; “Resume or Curriculum Vitae”; “Research Statement (1-2 pages)”; “Submit as "Supplemental Document 1."”
> “A scanned copy of your official transcript from each post-secondary institution attended is required.”
> “Three Letters of Recommendation”; “Be sure to include letters from your research advisor(s)”
> “The minimum TOEFL iBT score required for consideration to the IBiS program is 4.5 on the 1–6 scoring scale for exams taken on or after January 21, 2026. For exams taken before January 21, 2026, the minimum score is 90 on the 0–120 scoring scale.”
> “The minimum IELTS score required for admission is 7.0. The minimum MET is 61.”
> “Earning an undergraduate or graduate degree from an accredited institution where the language of instruction is English exempts the applicant from having to take and submit TOEFL scores.”
> “Official scores must be no more than two years old at the time of intended matriculation”
> “Selected applicants will be invited to participate in interviews in late January and early February.”
> “Top applicants will then be invited to attend an in-person visit in March.”
> “Admissions decisions are finalized and offers are made soon after the interviews.”
> “Applicants who are not selected for an interview will receive a decision via CollegeNet by April 15th.”
> “Evanston, IL 60208”
