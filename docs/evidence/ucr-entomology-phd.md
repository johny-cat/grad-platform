## Requirements / Admissions

Bachelor’s in entomology, biological science, chemistry, biochemistry or suitable equivalent; strength in life sciences. Master’s typical but direct bachelor-to-PhD possible. Science/math/data courses recommended, not required in current page, superseding older FAQ hard prerequisites. Identify faculty and research/teaching/outreach experience. Old FAQ requires potential-advisor commitment for admission; current page permits major-professor selection by first-quarter end; confirm route, model omitted.

GRE not-required. Current deadline recorded exactly in JSON; dates from current program application section.

## Funding

Competitive fellowships/traineeships can waive nonresident tuition, explicitly valuable to internationals. All applicants automatically considered, support offers with admission; no guarantee. Late/non-fall support only if available. GAANN restricted to US citizens/permanent residents and financial need.
