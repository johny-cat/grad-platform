## Application instructions
https://biomed.med.ufl.edu/bms-admissions/gnv-help/
Sections: opening; Steps; Transcripts; Statement of Purpose; Letters of Recommendation; test scores; fee.
Exact anchor: “Three (3) Letters of Recommendation”.
The detailed instructions instead reject applications incomplete or submitted beyond November 1; use this safer complete deadline. Gainesville-only application: College of Medicine / Medical Sciences Doctor of Philosophy, fall term, then concentration; rotations may change concentration, up to three interests in statement. Jupiter requires a separate application. Unofficial transcripts suffice for review; official all-institution records sent to Admissions. International records include native-language marksheets/degree certificates and certified literal translations. Statement maximum one page, single-spaced, half-inch margins. Three signed letterhead references, ideally faculty, with research-supervisor letter expected. GRE not required; program offers no fee waivers. Program legacy TOEFL internet/Home Edition 80 and IELTS 6.5; official results are the safer requirement because unofficial-score language conflicts internally.

## Funding
https://biomed.med.ufl.edu/financial-information/
Sections: Stipends & Tuition Waivers; Health Insurance. Exact anchor: “both domestic and international students”.
All admitted domestic/international students become graduate research assistants: $37,000 annually as of Fall 2026, tuition and fees covered. College funds the first two semesters; mentor grants/training grants usually fund later support, subject to satisfactory progress. Teaching is not required. Additional employment prohibited except Reserve/National Guard duty. Individual health coverage has a $12 monthly employee premium, university paying the balance.
