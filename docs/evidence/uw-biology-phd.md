## Common fee and English
https://grad.uw.edu/prospective-students/how-to-apply/apply-now/
- "The nonrefundable application fee is $90.00 (USD)".
https://grad.uw.edu/prospective-students/how-to-apply/application-fee-waivers/
- "Applicants who expect to hold or currently hold F-1 or J-1 visa status are not eligible for this fee waiver due to federal eligibility requirements."
- For stacked-degree-only waiver: "This fee waiver is available to domestic and international applicants, including those applying for F-1 or J-1 visas." This does not establish that these PhDs participate in an approved stacked pathway.
https://grad.uw.edu/policies/3-2-graduate-school-english-language-proficiency-requirements/
- Minimum table: TOEFL "4.0 (80 for tests taken before Jan 21, 2026)"; Duolingo "115"; Academic IELTS "6.5".
- Recommended table: TOEFL "4.5 (92 for tests taken before Jan 21, 2026)"; Duolingo "130 or higher"; IELTS "7.0 or higher".
- "An admitted applicant who scores below the Recommended level will be required to satisfactorily complete one or two UW Academic English Program (AEP) courses"; "additional fees apply".
- "An earned and bachelor’s, master’s, or doctoral degree (or an internationally recognized academic degree at the same level) from an accredited institution for which English was the medium of instruction."
- Residency must cover "the full duration of enrollment"; "Official documentation from the applicant’s degree-granting institutions verifying that all instruction is in English" is required except listed-country degrees.
- "Test scores are valid for two years and must be valid on the date the application is submitted".
- "TOEFL MyBest and IELTS One Skill Retake tests scores are not admissible".
