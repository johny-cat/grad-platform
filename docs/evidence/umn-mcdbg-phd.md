### https://cbs.umn.edu/gcd/graduate-programs/mcdbg-graduate-program/admissions

> [source line 33] Applications must be submitted to the University of Minnesota Graduate School through the Slate online system. Please see the Application Checklist for a complete list of required materials.

> [source line 35] Prospective students should apply to MCDB&G.

> [source line 36] *Note: MCDB&G and BMBB are two distinct graduate programs. Students accepted into the MCDB&G graduate program can rotate through research labs from either the MCDB&G or BMBB graduate program in the first year (see FAQ #15). MCDB&G and BMBB are two separate graduate programs with independent application review and admission processes. As such, students who wish to be considered for admission into both programs need to apply to each program (MCDB&G and BMBB) independently.

> [source line 37] If you do apply to both, please indicate that in your personal statement.

> [source line 38] ### Application Checklist:

> [source line 40]   * O nline application

> [source line 42]   * Application fee ($95.00 international, $75.00 domestic)

> [source line 44]   * Unofficial transcripts (official transcripts will be required if you are offered admission)

> [source line 46]   * Personal Statement

> [source line 48]   * Three letters of recommendation

> [source line 50]   * CV or resume

> [source line 52]   * English Language test scores - TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo (international students only.)

> [source line 53] ### Optional materials

> [source line 55]   * Diversity Statement: If you would like to include a statement that identifies the distinctive characteristics and/or life experiences, such as successfully overcoming obstacles or hardships, and how that could contribute to the education and enhanced perspectives at the University, please upload a separate Diversity Statement with your application.

> [source line 56]   * Description of Research: If you decide to add a description of your research please include how you became interested in your area of research and what direction you might take in the future. Concisely describe what you were studying. Be sure to include which aspects of the research projects you extensively planned and developed. Also describe your contributions to the implementation of the project.

> [source line 57] Descriptions of Research should have minimal overlap with content in your Personal Statement and should be viewed as a complement and/or supplement to what is written in your Personal Statement.

> [source line 58] ### Reminders:

> [source line 59]   * Upload all materials, including transcripts, to the online application.

> [source line 60]   * Do not submit your application until all materials are uploaded.

> [source line 61]   * Do submit your application even if all of your letters of recommendations have not been submitted.

> [source line 62]   * Encourage all your referees to submit their letters online.

> [source line 63]   * Check your email frequently. If you are missing materials or if there are any problems with your application, we will contact you only by email.

> [source line 64]   * If you have a publication, please submit only the abstract.

> [source line 65] ### Application Timeline:

> [source line 67]   * December 1: Application deadline

> [source line 68]   * By January 15: Applications reviewed, candidates invited to recruiting visits

> [source line 69]   * Late January and early February: Recruiting visits

> [source line 70]   * During February: International applicants interviewed

> [source line 71]   * By the end of February: Offers begin to be made

### https://cbs.umn.edu/gcd/graduate-programs/mcdbg-graduate-program/financial-aid

> [source line 32] MCDB&G students in good standing are supported financially with a stipend plus full tuition, all student fees, and 95% of health insurance. Completion of financial aid forms is not necessary, even though the Graduate School lists these forms as being available.

> [source line 48] All full-time Ph.D. students receive a semester-by-semester appointment or fellowship. The rate of pay in the Fall of 2026 will be $36.77/hour. Tuition and some student fees are also covered for full-time Ph.D. students.
