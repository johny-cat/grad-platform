## Requirements; application selections; Transcripts; Supporting Documents; Standardized Exam Scores

Source: https://medicine.iu.edu/bloomington/graduate-programs/cell-molecular-and-cancer-biology-phd/admissions

Exact anchor: “No GRE scores required.”

Science bachelor’s equivalent; $70 fee; CV, three essay responses, three or more research-informed referee letters and all university transcripts. Unofficial review copies are allowed; the Transcripts section requires official copies before an official offer. Essays cover research, motivation, careers, program and faculty fit. Select Doctoral/PhD, IU Medical Science, and Cell, Molecular and Cancer Biology. Non-native speakers need TOEFL/IELTS; recommended legacy TOEFL 100 and IELTS 7.0. Exemption requires English-taught college attendance in a country where English is official or primary. International applicants are explicitly addressed. The page links central fee-waiver opportunities; see _iu-c4-central.md.

## Financial support

Source: https://medicine.iu.edu/bloomington/graduate-programs/cell-molecular-and-cancer-biology-phd/financial-support

Exact anchor: “Financial Support”

All admitted students receive academic-year and summer stipends, guaranteed five years, through teaching/research assistantships, training grants or fellowships, plus health insurance. No tuition promise or current stipend amount is established here.
