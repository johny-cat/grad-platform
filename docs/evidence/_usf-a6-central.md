# USF A6 shared sources

Checked 2026-10-29.

https://www.usf.edu/admissions/international/admission-information/graduate/academic-requirements.aspx
English Proficiency: legacy TOEFL 79/new 4.5, IELTS 6.5, DET 110, PTE 53, Essentials 8.5; scores within two years of entry. Qualifying US composition courses with B or higher may exempt. No blanket foreign English-medium exemption inferred.

https://www.usf.edu/admissions/graduate/next-steps/check-application-status.aspx
Complete Your Application: $30 fee or hardship request. Exact anchor: “request a fee waiver in cases of financial hardship”. Confirm international eligibility and conflicting department restrictions.

https://www.moffitt.org/education/research-education-and-training/office-of-graduate-affairs/admissions/
Opening, How to Apply and All Applications: biology/chemistry bachelor background, GPA 3, annual December 1 all-materials deadline; three letterhead references with contact details, two-page statement, CV and translated/evaluated records as needed. Unofficial review, official after offer; optional GRE. Non-English-country applicants without US bachelor need English evidence: IELTS 6.5/PTE 53/legacy TOEFL 79/GRE Verbal 153, valid two years before entry.

https://www.moffitt.org/education/research-education-and-training/office-of-graduate-affairs/funding-support/
Funding support: current first-year stipend 33,000 plus arrival bonus 4,000, second-year 37,000, third-year after qualifying 39,000; outside employment prohibited. Rates undated. Exact anchor: “Full tuition and fee payments and student health insurance are provided to all students.”
