Both campus applications required, cannot also apply to standalone UCSD Biological Sciences. Fall 2027 table: SDSU application December 1, 2026; SDSU official materials and supplement December 15, 2026; UCSD application/materials December 15, 2026. ## https://biology.sdsu.edu/doctoral/cell-molecular/application-procedure/
Steps 1–3: SDSU $70 nonrefundable fee, no Cal State Apply waiver; GRE suspended/not required. TOEFL 80 before January 21, 2026 or 4 after; IELTS 7, DET 120, two-year validity at filing-period start. Official records, degree proof and certified literal translations. SOP usually 1–3 pages, CV, three letters (SDSU up to five), transcript copies and English results. UCSD needs three letters. Anchor: “$70”.

## https://biology.sdsu.edu/doctoral/cell-molecular/admission-information/

Doctoral students receive tuition and financial support; unspecified amount/duration/international coverage. Anchor: “three laboratory rotations”.
