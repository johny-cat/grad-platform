

## Official details reviewed 2026-10-23

Source: https://dbm.rutgers.edu/graduate (opened 2026-10-23)

> The Microbial Biology Graduate Program at Rutgers University offers a diverse research and educational experience focused on microbial life processes and their applications.

Source: https://dbm.rutgers.edu/graduate/application (opened 2026-10-23)

> Microbial Biology Ph.D. applications are due by December 15

> Application fee waivers can be obtained by U.S. citizens who have been traditionally underrepresented in graduate education

> A Personal Statement telling us why you are interested in microbiology and describing your previous research experience.

> A CV is requested but not required.

> We will review your application with the unofficial transcripts and official transcripts are only needed if we offer you admission

> Three letters of reference are required.

> Rutgers accepts the TOEFL, IELTS, and Duolingo English Test (DET).

> GRE scores are NOT required. GRE scores are NOT taken into consideration by the admissions committee

> The Microbial Biology admissions committee meets in the middle of January to review the Ph.D. applicants.

Source: https://dbm.rutgers.edu/graduate/faqs (opened 2026-10-23)

> Contacting research advisors in advance is not required and will not influence admission into our graduate program. All first year Ph.D. students do three lab rotations.

> We only have Fall admission.

> we offer competitive fellowships to entering first year Ph.D. students.

> After their first year Ph.D. students in good standing recieve fellowships, research assistantships, and/or teaching assistanatships depending on the resources of their Ph.D. advisor.

> you will be notified of your status concerning admission and financial aid by February.

> You must submit both original (native) and official English translations.
