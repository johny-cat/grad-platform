## https://www.uaf.edu/academics/programs/doctorate/biological-sciences.php

> UAF’s Biological Sciences Ph.D. program offers a broad pathway to a rewarding career as a professional biologist. Whether you’re aiming to make an impact in academia, state and federal agencies, non-profit organizations, or industry, this program will equip you with the advanced knowledge and skills required in this field.

> Beyond becoming an expert in a specialized area of biology, you’ll also gain invaluable training in research design, data analysis, critical thinking, and essential professional competencies like scientific writing, public speaking and securing research funding.

> As a Ph.D. student, you’ll collaborate with a graduate advisory committee to develop a dissertation that makes a meaningful, original contribution to your field and plan a set of courses that meet your educational goals. Students with a research focus on wildlife have the option to add a concentration in Wildlife Biology and Conservation.

> ## Career Pathways

> The Ph.D. program is designed for students who are passionate about pursuing professional careers in research and university-level teaching. Our graduates have gone on to secure faculty positions at universities, both in the U.S. and internationally. They’ve also excelled as scientists at leading federal and state agencies, including the Alaska Department of Fish and Game, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, U.S. Forest Service, and the National Parks Service.

> Others have made contributions to environmental consulting firms and renowned non-profit organizations, like the American Board of Radiology, the Nature Conservancy, and the Smithsonian Institution. The Biological Sciences Ph.D. program sets the stage for an impactful career across a wide range of recognized organizations.

## https://www.uaf.edu/bw/graduate/index.php

> Graduate degrees in the Biology and Wildlife Department prepare students for careers as professional biologists, whether in academia, state and federal agencies, non-profit organizations, or industry. In addition to gaining knowledge in a particular subdiscipline, graduate students receive training in research design and analysis, critical thinking, and professional skills such as scientific writing, oral communication, and grantsmanship. The department offers five graduate degree programs:

> MS in Biological Sciences

> MS in Wildlife Biology & Conservation

> PhD in Biological Sciences

> MS and PhD in Earth Systems Science Ecosystems

> All graduate degrees in Biology and Wildlife require a research thesis. Graduate students have access to many modern research facilities, including the DNA core lab, a state-of-the-art greenhouse, and the extensive resources of the UA Museum of the North. Students enjoy unparalleled access to field sites throughout Alaska, including the Arctic and Bonanza Creek (boreal) Long Term Ecological Research (LTER) sites.

> ##### Requirements for Acceptance into a B&W Graduate Program:

>   * There are no specific course prerequisites for entry into the programs

>   * However, students must have a strong academic record in an appropriate discipline and evidence of ability to complete a graduate degree

>   * Research experience is highly valued, especially for PhD applicants

>   * A Biology and Wildlife Department faculty member must agree to serve as the major advisor

>   * The department will identify a funding plan for you and your research prior to admittance

> Advisor requirements: your advisor or one of your co-advisors must be from within the Department of Biology & Wildlife. You can find a complete list of our department's faculty and sort through them by research area on our main faculty page.

> Prospective graduate students who are searching for a faculty advisor may consider folks from institutes and groups such as the ones below to serve as a co-advisor. They may have projects or expertise that align with your research goals. If you go this route, your other co-advisor must be faculty from the UAF Biology & Wildlife Department.

>   * University of Alaska Anchorage, Department of Biological Sciences

> The application deadline for fall admission is January 15. The deadline for spring admission is September 5. All application materials (including letters of recommendation) must be received before a file can be evaluated.

> ## Graduate Admission Requirements in Detail

> ### General Requirements

> In general, applicants may be admitted to a graduate program if they hold a bachelor's degree from an accredited institution with at least a 3.0 (B) cumulative undergraduate GPA and a 3.0 (B) GPA in their major field.

> Standard Required Materials:

>   * Resume or Curriculum Vitae

>   * Statement of Academic and Professional Goals

>   * Three official letters of recommendation

>   * GRE or GMAT scores (as required by program)

>   * $75 nonrefundable application fee

> Note: Your program may require additional materials such as licenses, writing samples, or a preliminary research prospectus.

> Check Catalog for Full Requirements

> ### Transcripts & Evaluation

> Official Transcripts: Required from all post-secondary institutions attended. Transcripts must be delivered directly in sealed institution envelopes or submitted electronically via a secure transcript service (e.g., Parchment, National Student Clearinghouse).

> In-Progress Coursework: Unofficial in-progress transcripts may be submitted for initial review, but final official transcripts are required upon admission.

> International Applicants: Must submit official academic records to The Evaluation Company (TEC) or World Education Services (WES) for a course-by-course credential evaluation. (Canadian institutions outside Quebec are exempt). Official TOEFL or IELTS scores are also required.

## https://www.uaa.alaska.edu/academics/college-of-arts-and-sciences/departments/biological-sciences/graduate/phd-biolsci.cshtml

> Students who wish to pursue a Ph.D. in Biological Sciences at the University of Alaska Anchorage may do so via collaborative programs with the University of Alaska Fairbanks. Students engaged in research with a UAA faculty member may apply to the following UAF Ph.D. programs: Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, Biology and Wildlife, or Fisheries and Ocean Sciences.

> A UAA faculty mentor can advise the applicant on which program is most appropriate for the course of study.  Each collaborative program has different admissions, degree, and residency requirements. Please visit the UAF Graduate School's website for additional information. We ask Ph.D.
