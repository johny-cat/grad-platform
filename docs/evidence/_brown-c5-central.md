Application Materials / Fee: personal statement, college records/certified translations, three online recommendations (two faculty from current/last institution), no Interfolio. Unofficial initial copies; official degree records before matriculation. Fee $75 each; separate applications for independent programs. Exact anchor: “The Graduate School requires three letters of recommendation”.

https://graduateschool.brown.edu/apply/application-fee-waivers
Fee Waiver Instructions: documented financial need, Additional Information request and explanation, only listed financial aid/FAFSA/CSS, last-90-day unemployment or GRE/TOEFL reduction-voucher evidence. Submit two weeks early recommended; provisional approval can reverse and application revert unsubmitted. No nationality restriction stated; overseas documentation eligibility not guaranteed. Exact anchor: “The Graduate School will only accept the documents listed above.”

https://graduateschool.brown.edu/apply/international-applicants/language-proficiency-toefl-or-ielts

US degree principally taught otherwise does not exempt. TA certification separate. Exact anchor: “recommended minimum overall band score is 7”.
