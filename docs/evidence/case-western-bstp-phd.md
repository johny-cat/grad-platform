

## Available official details reviewed 2026-10-23

Source: https://case.edu/medicine/bstp/ (opened 2026-10-23)

> The application for 2027 admission is open.

> The priority deadline is October 15th, and the final deadline is January 1st.

> Application fee waivers are not available to international students.

> US armed forces veterans, CWRU employees, students (including CWRU international students), and alumni can apply free of charge

Source: https://case.edu/medicine/bstp/admissions (opened 2026-10-23)

> Three letters of recommendation are required.

> The 1-2 page personal statement should include the experiences that shaped your interest in science and your long-term goals.

> Research Experience: List and describe all your research experiences.

> Application Fee: $85 USD paid at the time of submission.

Source: https://case.edu/medicine/bstp/admissions/frequently-asked-questions (opened 2026-10-23)

> We encourage prospective students to NOT contact faculty before submitting an application, or before being issued an admissions decision.

> applications lacking post-undergraduate research experience are unlikely to be considered for admission.

> Biology, organic chemistry, and mathematics through calculus/statistics are required, and biochemistry and molecular biology are strongly recommended.

> We do not require GRE scores, but we are happy to see the scores if you take the test.

> We do not have minimum grade requirements for admission

> At least one of these letters should come from a research mentor under whom you have performed independent scientific research.

Source: https://case.edu/programs/biomedical-sciences-training-program (opened 2026-10-23)

> As a BSTP student, you’ll complete at least three lab rotations

> School-issued unofficial transcripts, scanned and uploaded for all colleges or universities attended.

> Current resume/CV outlining all work experience and volunteer roles with timeframes.

> Three letters of recommendation, a personal statement, and separate written statements of your research and teaching experience.

Source: https://case.edu/medicine/bstp/admissions/stipend-and-benefits (opened 2026-10-23)

> All BSTP students are fully funded, meaning 100% tuition & fees are awarded to matriculating BSTP students.

> BSTP students earn a $38,000 stipend in the 2025-2026 academic year and $39,000 in the 2026-2027 academic year.

> Health insurance is provided for by the program.

> a $2,000 moving allowance is available to all matriculating students.
Source: https://case.edu/medicine/bstp/admissions/frequently-asked-questions (opened 2026-10-23)

> The goals statement is normally a paragraph to a page long.

> Recommendations must be submitted on official letterhead and signed by the recommender.

> Admissions decisions are generally issued within 1-2 weeks of the interview.
