# Baylor C5 central evidence
Checked 2026-10-29. Official sources opened once; concise factual paraphrases.

## Admission and dates
https://www.bcm.edu/education/graduate-school-of-biomedical-sciences/admissions
Important Dates: 2027 application open; December 1 early review, January 1 final, late space-available; program interviews January/February, offers February/March/April, response date in offer letter. Orientation July 28–30, 2027; classes August 2. Holistic Review has no GPA minimum; personal/research statements and recommendations evaluated. Exact anchor: “There is no application fee!”

https://education.bcm.edu/GSBS/Admissions/
Public login opening explicitly gives December 1, 2026 and January 1, 2027, but inconsistently calls this 2026–27 academic year. Current admissions page confirms 2027 entry.

## FAQ
https://www.bcm.edu/education/graduate-school-of-biomedical-sciences/admissions/frequently-asked-questions
International Applicants/Application Process/Test Scores: international GPA self-converted to 4.0; evaluations optional unless final transcript lacks major/degree/conferral. Unofficial records/scores permit review, official before matriculation. Recommendation requests follow submission; letters may arrive after deadline. No fee or GPA cutoff. Supervisor not required; three-to-five first-year rotations. GRE optional for Neuroscience/QCB, not required otherwise. Exact anchor: “No waiver request is necessary.”

## English and GRE
https://www.bcm.edu/education/graduate-school-of-biomedical-sciences/admissions/standardized-test-scores
English requirements: legacy TOEFL 90, new 5.0 from January 21, 2026; IELTS 6.5; DET 120 beginning 2027. Two-year validity, highest single test date, unofficial initial review but official scores before offer letter (stricter than FAQ timing). Accredited US degree, listed-country English-medium degree, or other foreign English-medium degree qualifies; latter needs sealed/stamped registrar/administrative confirmation of instruction and examination language. Programs may demand higher scores. Neuroscience/QCB optional GRE within five years. Exact anchor: “Scores cannot be older than two years.”

## Funding
https://www.bcm.edu/education/graduate-school-of-biomedical-sciences/admissions/stipends-benefits
Competitive Stipend/Teaching or Research/Student Health Insurance: PhDs receive stipend, full tuition remission and individual insurance; 2026–27 annual stipend $40,000, future amount unstated. Students pay named administrative/student fees; international additional fee. Teaching not required for stipend; family insurance student-paid. No explicit all-international guarantee asserted. Exact anchor: “teaching is not a requirement for earning a stipend.”

## Model
https://www.bcm.edu/education/graduate-school-of-biomedical-sciences/curriculum
Research Rotations: first year minimum three, usually four/five labs before mentor selection; any GSBS faculty rotation, external-home-program mentor requires approval. Exact anchor: “Students complete a minimum of three required rotations”.
