# Official evidence: Translational Biomedical Sciences PhD

Independently reopened and checked2026-10-28. Full relevant sentences and table entries are reproduced below. No private applicant information.

## https://biomedical.gsu.edu/admissions/graduate-students/doctoral-degree/

> A conferred bachelor's degree is the only requirement for applicants interested in applying for the Ph.D. program. All program prerequisites are built directly into the Ph.D. curriculum.
> How will you evaluate my application?
> Our admissions committee reviews each candidate’s entire application and weighs each piece equally, which we call a holistic review. We don’t eliminate applicants based solely on test scores or GPA.
> Students with lower GPAs can bolster their application by using their personal statement to explain why they earned the grades they did and how they plan to succeed in graduate-level courses should they be admitted, including a resume that describes their research experience, submitting a strong writing sample and having stellar recommendation letters.
> What is the minimum GPA and TOEFL/IELTS score required for admission into the Ph.D. program?
> Since each application undergoes a holistic review, we do not have required minimums.
> English Proficiency Minimums:
> TOEFL - 69 (or 4 on the 2026 scale)
> IELTS - 6.0 overall

> Graduate admissions application opens: August 1

> Application Fee Waivers
> IBMS offers a limited number of merit-based Application Fee Waivers. Waivers can be requested by filling out the Fee Waiver Request form.
> This form is for prospective students who are applying to an IBMS graduate program and would like to request a merit-based application fee waiver. Please be aware that IBMS fee waivers are limited in number and not guaranteed.
> To be eligible, applicants must:
>   * Have a cumulative grade point average of 3.8 or higher (on a 4.0 scale) in previous degree program(s)
>   * Have relevant wet-lab research experience
>   * Not qualify for application fee waivers offered by the Graduate School (see available waivers here)
>   * Start and submit their online application to an IBMS graduate degree program
>   * Submit this application fee waiver form and provide your transcript(s) and resume/CV for review
>   * If any of the criteria are not met, an application fee waiver cannot be awarded.

> You will need to submit academic transcripts from each college and/or university attended regardless of whether or not a degree was conferred.
> Note:
>   * Unofficial transcripts are sufficient for an admission decision.  If you are extended an offer of admission, you will need to submit official transcripts before the start of courses.
>   * Course-by-course credential evaluations are acceptable for an admission decision, however, transcripts will still be required in the event that you are admitted.
>   * Documents such as self-printed academic histories, web-based academic evaluations, screenshots or anything handwritten are not acceptable for an admission decision.
> 4. Submit Resume/CV & Personal Statement
> You will need to submit a resume/cv and a personal statement.
> Personal Statement - The personal statement is your opportunity to communicate who you are, your goals and your potential for being a good match for the Translational Biomedical Sciences program.

> 5. Submit a Writing Sample
> You will need to submit a writing sample. This document should be a substantive body of work that exhibits your ability to synthesize ideas, concepts or theories, logically draw conclusions and defend them.
> Note:
>   * Please do not submit a document that contains more than 20 pages. One to two pages is preferred.
>   * Examples of acceptable writing samples include an abstract, a general research paper, a capstone project, a master's thesis, a research proposal, an article review, a lab report, a case study or a term paper.
>   * An abstract is the preferred document.
> 6. Submit Letters of Recommendation
> You will need to submit at least 3 letters of recommendation with your application.  These letters should speak to your skill set and ability to be successful in a graduate program. Professional and/or academic letters are required.

> GRE scores are not required for admission.
> English Proficiency
> In addition to meeting all other applicable requirements for admission, non-native speakers of English must demonstrate sufficient English-language proficiency. Proof of English proficiency can be demonstrated through one of the following:
>   * Proof of baccalaureate or graduate degree completion inside (from an accredited institution) or outside the U.S., where English is the official language of academic instruction – see list of countries below
>   * TOEFL scores (paper or Internet-based)
>   * IELTS scores

> All accepted doctoral students will be awarded funding in the form of a graduate research assistantship upon admission.
> Doctoral applicants are not required to contact available faculty or secure a lab match before they are eligible to submit an application. At the start of the program, accepted doctoral students will be matched with 2-3 labs based on faculty interest, space availability and student research area preferences.
> To ensure satisfactory lab rotation placements, applicants should be sure to thoroughly and accurately complete the “Graduate Assistant” questions on the “Institute for Biomedical Sciences” tab of the application.
> It is strongly recommended that you thoroughly review the Faculty website, as well as PubMed and Google Scholar, to familiarize yourself with our most common research areas and current research projects happening within the Institute before completing this section of the application. Your answers will be used to assign lab rotations.

> Can I request an English proficiency waiver for my graduate application?[Input]
> IBMS does not currently offer English proficiency waivers. Applicants are expected to fulfill the English proficiency requirements as described by Graduate Admissions.
> Do I need to find a lab or faculty match before I can submit my application?[Input]
> No. Applicants are not required to find a faculty match/lab placement before applying to the program. After the applicant's educational credentials are vetted by the admissions committee and they are admitted, the student is either matched with 2-3 faculty members for 4-week rotations (doctoral students) or placed into a lab (master's students) at the start of their program.
> There is a section in the application that will ask you to indicate your interest in 3 IBMS faculty members, and we will use this information as a guide to assign rotations/lab placements.

> Is there funding available for Ph.D. students?[Input]
> All accepted doctoral students are awarded funding in the form of a graduate research assistantship (GRA) during the admissions process. GRAs work in one of our faculty member's labs an in exchange for a full tuition waiver and a monthly stipend of at least $2,500. This leaves the student responsible for mandatory student fees including health insurance and a $37.50 registration fee. All assistantship offers are dependent on the student's ability to successfully verify legal presence.
> Where can I go for answers to immigration and visa questions?[Input]
> Contact the Office of International Student and Scholar Services if you have questions related to immigration regulations and studies. Visit https://isss.gsu.edu/about/contact/ for contact information.
> Should I submit a copy of my passport with my application materials?[Input]
> Yes. Submit a copy of your passport with your application materials.
