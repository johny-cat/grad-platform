## Application / requirements sections

Bachelor’s required, master’s unnecessary. Biology, chemistry, physics, calculus and statistics recommended. Name intended faculty; prior advisor approval required, no admission without designated advisor.

GRE optional. Official transcripts; student-issued copies can go to department temporarily while official records pending; Three recommendation letters; Statement of purpose and research interests; CV/resume, generally1–3pages.

Deadlines: Autumn application: November 15. Undated recurring dates only.

## Funding

Most students funded via20-hour/week TA/RA or university fellowship; undated approximate monthly rate2,897USD plus full tuition waiver and employer-supported benefits. No all-admitted guarantee or explicit international coverage established.
