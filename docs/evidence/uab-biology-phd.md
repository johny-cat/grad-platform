Exact anchor: “Our program does not have lab rotations.” Source 2, Application Fee and English: standard international/domestic fees 60/50; new TOEFL 4, IELTS 6.5, DET 120, two-year window, MyBest/One Skill Retake accepted; US degree with GPA 3 or listed citizenship exemptions. Department legacy TOEFL 100, IELTS 7 and DET 130 take precedence.
