

## Available official details reviewed 2026-10-23

> Admission for graduate study at Drexel University requires a four-year bachelor's degree from a regionally accredited institution in the United States or an equivalent international institution.

> regular admission typically requires a minimum cumulative grade point average of 3.0 (B) for undergraduate work.

> The average for any graduate work must be at least 3.0.
