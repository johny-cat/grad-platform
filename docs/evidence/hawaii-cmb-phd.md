## https://manoa.hawaii.edu/graduate/documentation-requirements/

> ### Application Fee

> Application fee is $100 for all applicants. Application fees are NON-REFUNDABLE and may not be used for an application submitted at a later date. Applicants must pay by credit card. Cash is NOT accepted.

> ### Transcripts

> What is an Unofficial Transcript?

> An unofficial transcript is a copy of your academic record that's used for an initial admissions review. Unlike an official transcript, it can be a student-generated copy that is uploaded to your application. It may also be a document that is emailed, faxed, hand-delivered, or mailed in an unsealed envelope from a university or college.

> Please upload an unofficial transcript from each post-secondary institution you've attended when you submit your application. This document will be used for our initial review of your academic record. If you are admitted, you will be required to submit an official transcript to finalize your admission.

## https://manoa.hawaii.edu/graduate/english-proficiency-exemption-eligibility/

> Most applicants whose native language is not English are required to take an English Language Proficiency test. We currently accept TOEFL iBT (including TOEFL iBT Home Edition),TOEFL Essentials, IELTS Academic (including IELTS Online), Duolingo English Test, and select Cambridge English Scale tests (C1 Advanced, C2 Proficiency). Scores from tests taken at home/online, when administered by the test provider following standard procedures, are accepted.

> Scores based on multiple test administrations (sometimes called "superscores"), such as TOEFL MyBest scores and the IELTS One Score Retake, are also accepted.

> Applicants may submit unofficial scores while applying for admission. However, admitted students are required to submit official scores in order to enroll at University of Hawai'i at Mānoa. If applicable, you must submit TOEFL MyBest scores and the IELTS One Score Retake. Official scores should be sent directly to Graduate Division Student Services. The Educational Testing Service (ETS) code for University of Hawai'i at Mānoa is 4867. Test scores cannot be more than two years old.

> ### Exemption Eligibility

> Applicants are exempt from the English proficiency exams if they are native speakers of English or have received within the last five years a bachelor's degree or advanced degree from an accredited/recognized college or university in the English Language Proficiency Exempt Countries.

## https://cmb.jabsom.hawaii.edu/graduate-program/faqs.html

>   *  What is the application fee?

> The application fee is $100.

>   * Does your department provide application fee waivers?

> No, we do not offer fee waivers.  The University of Hawaiʻi Graduate Division requires the application fee, and the CMB Graduate Program does not have the provision for application fee waivers. We understand that this may pose a challenge for some, and we truly appreciate your understanding of our current constraints.

>   * Is there a list available of professors accepting Ph.D. students?

> If you click on the CMB Graduate Faculty Directory tab, https://cmb.jabsom.hawaii.edu/graduate-program/faculty-directory.html, there is a list of our graduate faculty with their research interests.  Research assistantship positions are dependent on faculty funding and space availability. For more information, contact faculty members whose research interests align with your interests.

>   * Do I need to secure a supervisor in the department prior my application?

> No, acceptance is independent of having secured a research or teaching assistantship.  Please refer to the funding section below.

> Funding – PhD Students

>   * Does the CMB Program offer graduate assistantships?

>  We expect that all PhD students will complete their dissertation research in laboratories that can sponsor a research assistantship that may be occasionally substituted with a teaching assistantship.  Applicants are encouraged to reach out to the laboratory principal investigators (PI) regarding graduate research assistantships. A list of the CMB graduate program faculty can be found at:

> https://cmb.jabsom.hawaii.edu/graduate-program/faculty-directory.html

>  See the following page for further information. https://manoa.hawaii.edu/graduate/recruitment-appointment/

>   *  How much will I be paid?

> Graduate assistantships (GA, RA, TA) provide a salary with medical benefits and a tuition waiver.  GAs must serve for a minimum of three months to be eligible for health plan benefits.
