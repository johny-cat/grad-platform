> ## Key outcomes

> Our thesis and dissertation-based M.S. and Ph.D. programs are focused on providing individualized, competency-based education to equip our graduates with the skills, abilities and attitudes to pursue diverse careers related to biomedical sciences.

> During the first year, our students acquire a background in biomedical science, consisting of topics in biochemistry, molecular biology, cell biology, pharmacology, physiology and immunology. They also complete lab rotations to identify the mentor and lab for their thesis or dissertation work. Students also complete discipline-specific advanced courses and electives based on their needs and academic discipline.

> All students in our Ph.D. in Biomedical Sciences program receive a benefits package that includes a stipend of $35,000 per year, plus full tuition, fees and health insurance. Students also have opportunities to apply for federal student aid, training grants, fellowships and scholarships through the Financial Aid Office. Students are encouraged to apply by the Dec. 1 priority deadline in order to maximize funding opportunities.

> International students may also qualify for support with the proper documentation.

>   * Statement of purpose outlining your research interests, career goals and reasons for applying

> At UNT Health, the Ph.D. in Biomedical Sciences program offers a variety of specialization areas that allow you to tailor your research in order to align with your interests and career goals. These concentrations are designed in order to provide rigorous training while preparing you for advanced roles in academic, clinical and industry settings.

> #### Specialized disciplines to choose from

> You can focus your Ph.D. studies in one of the following research-intensive disciplines:

>   * Biochemistry and Cancer Biology: Investigate the genetic, molecular and cellular mechanisms of cancer development and progression.

>   * Cell Biology, Immunology and Microbiology: Explore the cellular and molecular mechanisms that regulate immune responses and microbial interactions.

>   * Genetics: Dive deep into the genetic and epigenetic factors that influence human development and disease.

>   * Integrative Physiology: Investigate how organ systems function in health and disease with an emphasis on integrative, translational and systems-level research.

>   * Pharmaceutical Sciences and Pharmacotherapy: Advance drug discovery and patient care by researching drug mechanisms, delivery systems and therapeutic strategies.

>   * Pharmacology and Neuroscience: Study the interaction between chemical compounds and the nervous system to develop therapies for neurological and psychiatric disorders.

> ## Official transcript(s) from every college/university attended

>   * Please note, all transcripts are required. If you took one course at a community college or participated in a study abroad opportunity, we will need a transcript from the college where you completed the class.

>   * If you are still enrolled in classes, please request a transcript from your current institution after grades for the fall semester post to your record. We will also need to see your Spring registration (if you are enrolled for Spring)

>   * If you attended a college/university outside the U.S. you must submit an official WES course-by-course evaluation.
