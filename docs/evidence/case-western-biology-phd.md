

## Available official details reviewed 2026-10-23

Source: https://biology.case.edu/graduate/admission/ (opened 2026-10-23)

> Bachelor’s degree (typically a four-year degree), preferably with a major in sciences.

> A minimum cumulative grade point average of 3.0 (out of a 4.0 scale)

> Prior research and/or teaching experience in the field of biological sciences or a relevant field

> Statement of Objectives: 1–2 pages document that addresses your research experience and academic goals.

> Resume/CV

> Unofficial copies are OK. Official transcripts are required only if you are accepted.

> Three letters of recommendation

> $85.00 application fee (non-refundable)

> GRE is NOT required.

> December 15th for full consideration into PhD, MS Plan A (thesis), and MS Plan B (non-thesis).

Source: https://biology.case.edu/graduate/financial-aid/ (opened 2026-10-23)

> The Department of Biology provides available financial support through teaching assistantships, research assistantships, and/or monthly stipends to qualified graduate students.

> Financial aid offers are competitive and based on the availability of funding each year.
Source: https://biology.case.edu/graduate/phd/ (opened 2026-10-23)
