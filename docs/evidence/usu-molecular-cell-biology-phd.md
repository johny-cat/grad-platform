> PHD in Molecular and Cell Biology

> ## ABOUT THE PROGRAM

> Modern biology has been revolutionized by developments in molecular and cell biology. These developments cross traditional disciplines in ways that involve virtually every aspect of biomedical investigation. Understanding the complex mechanisms involved in cell-cell communication, intracellular signal transduction, regulation of gene expression, and immune response to foreign antigens and pathogens all require the application of multidisciplinary methods and thinking.

> Similarly, the investigation of the basis for the structural organization of cells and their organelles, the control of cell division, and the processes that lead to oncogenic transformation is most productive when techniques and ideas from diverse disciplines are brought to bear, as is the rational design of appropriate strategies and therapeutic agents to combat human disease.

> The ability to apply a multitude of approaches to a single problem and to exploit the advantages of diverse model systems is the hallmark of interdisciplinary research.

> The Molecular and Cell Biology (MCB) PhD program is designed for full-time students with strong backgrounds in biology and chemistry, and a desire to explore the molecular basis of biological processes and human disease. We provide students with an educational experience that nurtures their ability to develop as professional scientists and carry out research that draws expertise and tools from multiple resources across disciplines.

> The academic environment strongly promotes networking, interpersonal and leadership skills and facilitates collaboration between military and civilian scientists and students involved in research projects spanning both basic and clinical science disciplines.

> The MCB faculty are drawn from several different basic science and clinical departments across the university. Their research expertise spans a wide range of areas including immunology, genetics, biochemistry, genomics, regulation of gene expression, virology, structural biology, cancer biology and military medicine.

> Their research attracts extensive funding from the National Institutes of Health, the National Science Foundation, and a host of other public and private agencies and provide the MCB students with the opportunity to receive training that prepares them for careers in academic, government, or industry research settings.

> Students receive a comprehensive didactic and laboratory training in all areas of contemporary biomedical research. Course work involves lectures and discussion classes on both fundamental and advanced areas of molecular and cell biology and lab rotations following which the students transition to original laboratory research leading to their PhD degree.

## https://medschool.usuhs.edu/geo/admissions

> This information is for individuals interested in full-time in-person graduate degree programs at USU. Instructions for graduate certificates and online programs will be posted when applications open in September 2026.

> #### 1. Welcome & Overview

>   * Welcome to Graduate Admissions at the USU School of Medicine!  Our mission is to drive scientific innovation by creating a pipeline of military and civilian clinicians, scientists and leaders dedicated to mission-driven research, force readiness, and safeguarding the health of the United States.

>   * You can learn more about specific USU graduate programs here, and in the USU online catalog (select current academic year at top right).

>   * USU gives careful consideration to all available information about each applicant and selects students on a competitive basis without regard to race, color, sex, creed, or national origin.

>   * There is no application fee and no tuition.

> #### 2. Conditions for Applying

>   * Applicants must be US citizens.

>   * Each applicant must complete a baccalaureate degree program from an accredited academic institution before matriculation at USU.

>   * Eligibility varies by program, so please check your eligibility before applying:  Admission Process

>   * Part 1: Basic Demographics

>   * Complete the initial online application to gather demographics and register with the USU Registrar. APPLICATION PART 1

>   * If you do not receive your student ID within 2-3 business days, please check your spam folder and then contact universityadmissions@usuhs.edu.

>   * Allow 1-2 business days after submitting Part 1 to receive your Student ID via email. This ID is required to complete Parts 2 and 3 of the application.

>   * Please note that you cannot fill out Part II of the application without a student ID number. This number will be provided to you by the Office of the Registrar when they email you a copy of your application form. Please allow at least 24 hours of processing time to receive this email and your student ID number.

>   * Part 2: Supplemental Information

>     * Complete the required Google Form to provide supplemental program information. APPLICATION PART 2

>   * Part 3: Supporting Documents

>   * You must be logged into your Google or Google-linked account in order to submit this form.

>   * Complete the required Google Form to upload required documents. APPLICATION PART 3

>   * Applicants must complete Part 1 and Part 2 before finalizing their application in Part 3.

>   * Be sure to input the same Email Address and Student ID assigned to you each time you submit documents.

>   * Required for all applicants: A Statement of Purpose and a complete Curriculum Vitae or Resume (please remove your street address).

>   * All documents do not have to be uploaded in one submission; multiple submissions can be made as documents are ready.

>   * If you are unable to upload supporting documents in Part 3, please contact UniversityAdmissions@usuhs.edu for assistance.

> #### 4. Official Documents & Test Scores

>   * Official Transcripts & Letters of Recommendation:

>     * Three letters of recommendation must be submitted directly from the recommender to USU. USU’s Registrar will contact references listed on your application with instructions.  Please let your references know to expect this email.

>     * Direct all Official Transcripts to the USU Registrar at UniversityAdmissions@usuhs.edu.

>     * Electronic transcripts are only accepted from a secure site formally linked from the sending institution (schools located in the United States only).

>   * GRE Requirements:

>     * The GRE is required only for those applying to the Medical & Clinical Psychology Graduate Programs. Test scores from within the last 5 years will be accepted.

>     * For all other applicants, the GRE is optional for the 2026-2027 admissions cycle. To submit optional scores, the test must have been taken within three years.

>     * USU's school code for the GRE is 5824.

> #### 5. Application Deadlines

>   * November 1: PhD in Medical Psychology & PhD in Clinical Psychology.

>   * December 1: Most PhD and Master’s Programs.
