## Application / requirements sections

Minimum three different7-week rotations; join lab in mid-spring. Letters may arrive after deadline but no review until complete. International applicants abroad interview remotely; visitations February, most decisions mid-March, acceptance dueApril15.

GRE optional. Statement of intent: biochemistry motivation, research impact/interests, faculty fit, goals, activities and any academic anomalies; CV/resume; Three recommendation letters; Upload official transcripts from all institutions.

Deadlines: Domestic and international application: December 1. Undated recurring dates only.

## Funding

All admitted OSBP students guaranteed support with satisfactory standing/progress: full tuition, academic fees, health insurance and stipend; first year GRA/fellowship then advisor. Funding page lists historical2023–24 stipend32,352USD/year, not current rate.
