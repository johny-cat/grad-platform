

## Available official details reviewed 2026-10-23

Source: https://case.edu/gradstudies/prospective-students/instructions-requirements (opened 2026-10-23)

> All applicants must have the equivalent of a four-year, US bachelor’s degree conferred prior to the start of the term of admission.

> Identify and upload an unofficial transcript from each post-secondary institution directly onto the application.

> If applicable, pay your non-refundable $85 application fee.

> If you are experiencing a severe financial hardship which prevents you from paying the application fee, please contact your program of interest to see about an application fee waiver.

> Fee waivers are specific to programs and are not to be shared, and not guaranteed to be available.

Source: https://case.edu/gradstudies/prospective-students/international-applicant-info (opened 2026-10-23)

> for TOEFL exams taken on or after January 21, 2026, the minimum score is 5.0

> For exams taken on or before January 20, 2026, the minimum score is 90

> paper-based: 577.

> International English Language Testing System (IELTS): 7.0

> Pearson Test of English (PTE-Academic): 61

> all of these scores are valid for 2 years from the date of testing

> We are currently accepting the TOEFL Home Edition.

> The School of Graduate Studies does not accept the Duolingo exam, the IELTS Indicator, the IELTS One Skill Retake, TOEFL Essentials exam, or TOEFL MyBest Scores.

> The language test requirement will be waived if you have completed a bachelor’s or master’s degree from an institution where English is the primary language of instruction.
