> [source line 79] ## Applications for Fall 2027 are due December 1, 2026.

> [source line 80] ### Application portal: Go to the Graduate School Admissions Online Application form at SLATE Application . Read the Application Procedure section below thoroughly and check out our FAQ to find answers to your questions.

> [source line 81] IMPORTANT MESSAGE FOR INTERNATIONAL APPLICANTS: For the 2027-28 academic year (December 1, 2026 application deadline) the Bioscience PhD Programs and the Interdepartmental Program in Neuroscience will limit the consideration of international student applications to only those individuals currently in the United States holding valid nonimmigrant status. Those who are outside the U.S. will not be eligible for consideration. This is a temporary shift in our application review process.

> [source line 93] To avoid confusion and delays in processing applications, you can only apply to one of the two Programs: Molecular Biology PHD or Biological Chemistry PHD for Fall 2027.  See our Program Comparison page for more details.

> [source line 94] The application fee for the Molecular Biology PHD or Biological Chemistry PHD is $55 for domestic applicants and $65 for international applicants.

> [source line 95]   * The application fee must be paid in order to consider your application complete.

> [source line 96]   * Application fee waiver codes are program specific and data entry errors or codes created by other programs will not be honored or credited.

> [source line 97]   * Eligible applicants may enter a program specific application fee waiver code in the field at the bottom of the Additional Materials page.

> [source line 98]     * Fall 2027 Domestic applicant code MBBCUTAHPHD

> [source line 99]     * International applicant fee waiver is not available and International applicants the outside the U.S. will not be eligible for consideration for Fall 2027.

> [source line 100]   * The University of Utah and the Bioscience PhD Programs cannot refund or reverse application fees paid by the applicant.

> [source line 101]   * Faculty and departments are not able to provide or sponsor application fee waivers for international students.

> [source line 106] The GRE will no longer be required or accepted.

> [source line 119] Competitive applicants should have a science background and research experience.

> [source line 120] All portions of the application packet carry equal weight.  However, shortcomings in one area of the application may be balanced by strengths in another. Meaningful research experience for which the applicant had primary responsibility is one of the best ways to strengthen an application.

> [source line 129] When uploading your documents DO NOT upload items that are not requested (i.e. awards, certificates, publications, etc.).

> [source line 139] Under Academic Program select either "Biological Chemistry PHD" or "Molecular Biology PHD". The Entry Term and Year is "Fall 2027".  Students should identify the Program that best accommodates their research interests and provides the most appropriate core curriculum. Some of the most important similarities and differences between the MB and BC Programs are highlighted in our detailed Program Comparison page. Please be aware that you can only apply to the Programs a total of three times.

> [source line 140] List ten (10) faculty members whose research is of interest to you. Select in order of preference. See the Bioscience Faculty Index. Although these choices are not binding for rotations or thesis study this information helps guide the Admissions Committee and can be used as first choice to schedule virtual or in person interviews.

> [source line 141] To upload paper materials you need to scan them with a scanner/printer, save them in .pdf file format, and then upload the files in the appropriate place.  If you have several pages for your transcript, for example, then you will scan each page then merge all pages in to one document and then save.  You can reduce document size or optimize scanned .pdf file to make the document smaller. These functions can be found under the document tab of Adobe Acrobat.

> [source line 142] Do not attempt to upload a document that is password-protected or that contains macros.

> [source line 143] ### icon Transcripts

> [source line 145] In the Academic History section of the Slate application, please upload .pdf copies of ALL of your official transcripts from each college or university attended (college/university level only). This includes transcripts from the original institution prior to transfer of credits to another institution, such as community college classes transferred to a university will require two separate uploads.

> [source line 164] In the “Additional Materials” section in the Slate application upload a .pdf copy your CV in the CV/Resume field. A sample CV can be viewed here.

> [source line 165] ### icon Research Statement

> [source line 167] In the “Additional Materials” section of the Slate application, a research statement (2-3 pages) should be uploaded. It should be articulate and give evidence of a strong commitment to research. Please include the following information:

> [source line 168]   1. A description of your present academic interests and long-range career goals

> [source line 169]   2. A summary of previous experience including laboratory research

> [source line 170]   3. Reasons for applying to the program

> [source line 171]   4. A description of your scientific interests and a list of faculty whose research interests are compatible with your own. (Although it is not essential to have specific research interests at this point, it is important that students admitted to the Program have several potential research advisors. Details about participating faculty can be found here).

> [source line 172] ### icon Research Experience

> [source line 174] Enter the number of months of full-time research experience in the “Additional Materials” section. Research months or Person months is an important metric to demonstrate research experience.

> [source line 181] In the “Recommendations” section, please add the contact information for three (3) letters of recommendation writers. Add people familiar with your research potential. Three (3) letters of recommendation are required, but up to four (4) may be submitted. The letters must be on letterhead, have a valid signature, and be submitted through the Slate Recommendation system.

> [source line 182] Your recommenders must submit your letters of recommendation by the December 1 deadline. Please note that notification emails will indicate "The University of Utah Office of Admissions" as the sender and will come from admissions@utah.edu. If your recommenders use a spam-blocking tool, please ask them to add this email address to their list of known/safe addresses.
