# Neuroscience PhD — evidence

Checked 2026-10-29. Official sources only. Facts below are paraphrases tied to the named official sections; quoted anchors are brief exact text, not full-sentence transcriptions.

## Graduate Application Components; English; deadline; double majors

Source: https://neuroscience.indiana.edu/academics/how-to-apply/index.html

Exact anchor: “The deadline for all applications is December 1”

Fall-only PhD application: statement, CV, three letters, UG transcript. Official domestic records directly to program; international records directly to international admissions. English minima legacy TOEFL 79, IELTS 6.5, DET 100; DET does not establish teaching ability, TEPAIC may be required. December 1 has no year. Faculty contact encouraged. Double majors need separate applications and yield one doctorate.

## Decision timing; English exemption; multiple departments

Source: https://neuroscience.indiana.edu/academics/how-to-apply/faq.html

Exact anchor: “you will only be charged one application fee”

Decisions typically mid-February–March. US undergraduate degree automatically exempts TOEFL/IELTS. Psychology/Cognitive Science/Neuroscience share materials and charge one fee for multiple applications, following stated application-copy instructions. No fee amount is given.

## Eligibility for the Ph.D. Program in Neuroscience

Source: https://neuroscience.indiana.edu/academics/eligibility/index.html

Exact anchor: “Students holding a bachelor degree are eligible to apply for admission.”

Bachelor’s eligible; strong natural-science background, research and strong recommendations valued. Dual major requires admission to each program.

## Funding

Source: https://neuroscience.indiana.edu/academics/funding/index.html

Exact anchor: “Funding”

Full-time graduate students typically receive fellowships/assistantships with 100% tuition remission; assistantships require 20 hours weekly. No all-student guarantee or current stipend amount established.
