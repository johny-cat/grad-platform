> ## Requirements

> ### Transcripts

>     * Domestic Transcripts: You may submit unofficial transcripts with your application. Official transcripts will be required upon admission to a graduate program.

>     * International Transcripts: Must be evaluated through a course-by-course transcript evaluation from a NACES-approved organization (e.g., ECE, TEC - formerly SpanTran, WES). The evaluation must include an original copy of your official transcript as received by the evaluating agency. Please allow adequate time for evaluation processing (typically 8+ weeks).

> * * *

> ### Recommendations

>     * 3 recommendations to be submitted through the online application

>       *  Please include references from individuals who are able to describe your research abilities and/or academic preparedness for graduate school

>   * General International Admissions Requirements

> NOTE: All International applicants must utilize a NACES approved organization to have your transcripts evaluated. We receive a high volume of International applications and our internal evaluation process can take approximately 8+ weeks. Please plan ahead as this may impact application deadlines.

> Some of the frequently utilized organizations are IEE (International Education Evaluations, Inc.) ECE (Educational Credential Evaluators, inc), and WES (World Education Services, inc), but you may utilize any approved organization.

> Individual program requirements are additional to the following:

>     * Have the minimum equivalent of a four-year bachelor’s degree with at least a “B” average

> based on the home country’s grading scale.

>       * European students with three-year Bologna bachelor’s degrees and Indian students with three-year bachelor’s degrees from Indian universities ranked A (3.01- 4.0) through the National Assessmentand Accreditation Council (NAAC) accreditation process will be considered to have equivalent four-year degrees.

>     * Applicants must meet the following GPA requirement:

>       * 3.0 on a 4.0 scale.

>     * A completed admissions application

>     * Individual programs may have specific instructions for the content and format of your statement. Please be sure to check with their application instructions for details. If specific instructions for your program do not exist, the personal statement should cover why you want to go to graduate school and why this program would be a good fit with your career goals. The statement should be double-spaced with minimum one inch (2.54 cm) margins, minimum 12 point font, and 1-2 pages in length.

>     * Submit the non-refundable application fee of $65.00.

>     *       * The application fee must be submitted before any further application materials can be sent to the graduate school.

> Please note that the Graduate School does not provide application fee waivers. The application fee is a required processing fee. The only exception to this is if you are a currently enrolled graduate student at ETSU.

## https://www.etsu.edu/com/research/graduate.php

> Studies lead to a Ph.D. in Biomedical Science in one of five concentrations:

>   * Cardiovascular Sciences

>   * Cellular, Molecular & Chemical Biology

>   * Immunology, Inflammation & Infectious Diseases

>   * Neuroscience

>   * Pharmaceutical Sciences

> Students receive support through graduate assistantships that include a stipend.  Tuition and fees are also paid for the student.  Currently, a stipend of $27,000 per year is provided to students in the Biomedical Science Graduate Program. Travel awards and training in teaching are also available for students.
