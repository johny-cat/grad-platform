## Deadline and materials

https://ils.uga.edu/admissions/how-to-apply/ — Application Deadline, Application Process, Statement of Purpose, Acceptance Notification Timeline.

Exact deadline: “11:59 PM EST on December 1, 2026”. Page explicitly identifies Fall 2027. All components and supplemental questions must be complete; scores/exemptions take 2–4 weeks to verify. Application choice is Athens, Doctoral, PHD_ILSC. Checklist requires fee, transcripts, three letters, CV, statement and verified English evidence when applicable. Supplemental choices: three groups, departments and faculty each. Statement maximum 10,000 characters; prompts cover motives, interests, scholarship and contextual circumstances. No writing sample/publication copies. Submit before letters if needed, but finish by deadline. Screening December/early January; international video interviews January/February and domestic recruitment late January/February; offers follow interviews.

## FAQ: GRE, mentor, waivers, international applicants and funding

https://ils.uga.edu/admissions/faq/ — corresponding question headings.

Exact fragments: “including certain international student exchange programs”; “Stipend support in years 2 through 5 is guaranteed”.

GRE may be supplemental; no mentor commitment before application. International accredited undergraduate degrees accepted subject to central country credentials. No ILS waiver, but limited Graduate School qualifying-program waivers, including the international exchange category above. First semester funded without teaching. Later RA/TA/fellowships and home-program stipend apply. Tuition reduction is conditional on assistantship; insurance subsidized. Speaking recommendations: TOEFL 22/IELTS6.5; lower scores may require English courses. Admission averages are descriptive, not thresholds.

## Stipend and tuition qualifications

https://ils.uga.edu/admissions/stipends/ — Graduate Student Stipends; Other Financial Support; Teaching Requirement.

Exact fragment: “For the Fall 2025 program year, the stipend is $34,738.” It is not a Fall 2027 amount. Support carries into first spring, then resets to home-unit standard in year two. Tuition remitted for year one and afterward with RA/TA; FAQ calls it reduced, so conflict is disclosed. Insurance subsidized. First fall has no teaching; later requirements depend on funding/unit/adviser.
