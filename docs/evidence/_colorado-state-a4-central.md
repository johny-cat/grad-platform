# Colorado State central policies — A4
Checked 2026-10-28.

https://graduateschool.colostate.edu/apply/ — Domestic and International application requirements. Fee table: domestic $60, international degree $70; nonrefundable. Degree equivalent to U.S. bachelor required by entry. Transcripts, CV, statement and recommendations; departments specify two or three. Standard regular GPA 3.0.

https://graduateschool.colostate.edu/skip-the-application-fee/ — Fee waivers and instructions. Qualifying U.S. land-grant/Colorado degrees automatic; military, Peace Corps, AmeriCorps and Fulbright affiliations need documentation. Submit application, await waiver link, request without paying; allow four business days, apply a week early. Older 2024/2025 free days are not current offers.

https://graduateschool.colostate.edu/admissions-resources/english-proficiency/ — Minimum Test Scores table and exemptions. TOEFL80 before/4.5 after January2026, IELTS6.5, DET120, PTE58, agency scores within two years. Country citizenship list/sole-English official language or recent U.S./listed-country degree exemptions; native-language-taught program waiver. Higher departmental scores possible. No blanket overseas English-medium exemption inferred.
