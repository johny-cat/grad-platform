# IU Bloomington C4 central application-fee evidence

Checked 2026-10-29. Source: https://bloomington.iu.edu/admissions/apply/graduate/application-fees.html

Sections: Fee waiver eligibility; Eligibility criteria; Fee waiver options based on application status; Fee waiver options based on membership, recruitment programs, or volunteer experience.

Exact anchor: “Fee waiver options based on application status”

The Graduate School handles its own programs only. General categories include named IU recruitment participation, qualifying service, military status and highly competitive multiyear fellowships with annual stipend of at least $20,000. Application-status routes include one fee for dual programs and $35 for qualifying current-student/previous-application cases; request a code with reasons. General/status sections do not expressly restrict nationality, so availability is conditional and international applicants must confirm eligibility. Separate membership/recruitment/volunteer routes explicitly restrict eligibility to US citizens/permanent residents, including BTAA and listed conference routes. Do not describe these as general hardship or automatically international waivers. No base fee amount established centrally; Biology and CMCB state their own $70 fees. SOPHAS-specific fees are not established by this page.
