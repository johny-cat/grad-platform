## Application / requirements sections

Summer entry only: select Summer in application. Natural-science undergraduate preparation preferred, including biology, chemistry/biochemistry, physiology, genetics, physics and calculus; research experience required, one hypothesis-driven year preferred. GPA3.0 minimum. International applicants must secure faculty commitment to stipend, tuition and fees for5+years before applying; FAQ also describes rare admission without this, so confirm. International route differs from first-year domestic cohort support.

GRE not-required. Personal statement (generally one page) on background, research and career goals; CV/resume; Reference letters submitted through system; count not established on opened pages; Scans of official transcripts for review; official complete records and degrees after offer.

Deadlines: Recommended summer application: December 1; Final summer application: February 1. Undated recurring dates only.

## Funding

All incoming students first-year stipend/tuition/fees commitment on admissions overview; FAQ excludes internationals from program first-year funds and requires their own faculty sponsor for5+years. International coverage depends on secured sponsor. No unconditional program funding promise to internationals.
