## Page extracts

### https://gradschool.princeton.edu/admission-onboarding/prepare/application-deadlines
- "Prospective applicants are encouraged to explore our fields of study to confirm deadline information and specific requirements by academic department or program."
- "The application for Fall 2027 is now open."
- "Your application and all required supporting materials must be submitted by the deadline listed below for the program to which you are applying."
- "late applications are not accepted."
- "All applications must be submitted by 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the posted deadline date."
- "While the application is for Fall 2027 enrollment, all associated deadlines fall within the 2026 calendar year (e.g., November 15, 2026) and are subject to change until September 1."
- Table (deadline | fields): "November 15*" Psychology ; "December 1" Art and Archeology, Bioengineering, Biophysics, Chemical and Biological Engineering, Chemistry, Classics, Comparative Literature*, East Asian Studies, Economics, Ecology and Evolutionary Biology, English, History, History of Science, Materials Science Engineering, Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Molecular Biology, Neuroscience*, Princeton School of Public and International Affairs*, Program in Plasma Physics, Quantitative and Computational Biology ; "December 15" and "December 21*" list other fields.
- "*Indicates a change in the application deadline from last year."
- "Please note, the following degree programs are not accepting applications for Fall 2027: Population Studies, Computer Science, M.S.E." (none of the four programs checked here)

### https://gradschool.princeton.edu/admission-onboarding/prepare/application-fee
- "A nonrefundable $90 application fee is required with your application. Fees are paid online through the application portal."
- "Applicants may be eligible to request a fee waiver based on the following criteria."
- Criteria headings: "Financial Hardship" ; "Participation in a Program" ("This can be past or present participation." ; "Only programs from the list below are accepted.") ; "Recruiting Event Attendee" ("you will be asked to enter the event fee waiver code you received." ; "Only events from the list provided on the application are accepted.") ; "Virtual Workshop Attendee"
- Participating programs include "McNair Scholars", "National Science Foundation - Research Experiences for Undergraduates (NSF-REU)", "QuestBridge Scholars", "IIE/Fulbright" and "U.S. Military".
- "We encourage you to submit your fee waiver request as soon as possible. The deadline to submit a fee waiver request is two business days before the department application deadline."
- "A fee waiver decision will be emailed to you within 2-3 business days. If your fee waiver request is denied, you will need to submit your application and pay the application fee by the deadline."
- The page does not say fees or waivers differ for international applicants.

### https://gradschool.princeton.edu/admission-onboarding/prepare/english-proficiency
- "If your primary language is not English, you must meet English language proficiency requirements and may be required to submit TOEFL, IELTS, or DET test scores."
- "Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL)" ; "International English Language Testing System (IELTS)" ; "Duolingo English Test (DET)"
- "The Graduate School does not have a minimum score requirement. Please review the Fields of Study page for department specific requirements."
- "Send test scores no later than three weeks before the degree program application deadline to ensure receipt of the official score report."
- "no offers of admission will be made if required scores are not in the application."
- Exemptions ("The following applicants are not required to submit English language test scores. All other applicants must submit valid TOEFL, IELTS or DET scores:"): "Applicants whose primary language is English." ; "Applicants whose undergraduate instruction is entirely in English as certified by the institution and for whom the degree will be awarded prior to anticipated enrollment at Princeton." ; "Applicants whose graduate study was on a full-time basis for at least two academic years where instruction was entirely in English as certified by the institution." ; "Please note that this requirement must be fully met at the time the application opens to qualify for the exemption."
- "Beginning January 21, 2026, ETS introduced a new TOEFL iBT scoring scale (1-6). Applicants may submit TOEFL scores reported on either the new 1-6 scale or the previous 0-120 scale, and both formats will be accepted for admission review."
- "Beginning with Fall 2026 admission, test scores will only be considered if they remain valid as of the date the application opens (September 15)."
- "Scores that expire before the application opening date—even if submitted earlier—will not be accepted."
- "If your scores are as follows you will be required to take an English language proficiency assessment upon enrollment regardless of if you qualified for an exemption:" ; "TOEFL (Speaking) of lower than 5.5 (on 1-6 scale) or 27 (on 0-120 scale)" ; "IELTS (Speaking) of lower than 8.0" ; "DET (Conversation) of lower than 125"

### https://gradschool.princeton.edu/admission-onboarding/prepare/gre
- "The Graduate School does not have a minimum score requirement; each department or program may set its own."
- Definitions: "Optional/Not Required: Applicants are not required to submit GRE scores as part of the application requirements for the department or program to which they are applying." ; "Applicants who do not submit scores will not be put at a disadvantage." ; "Not Accepted: Applicants do not need to submit GRE scores as part of the application requirements for the department or program to which they are applying." ; "If scores are submitted by an applicant, they will not be reviewed as part of the admission review process."
- Heading "GRE Table: Requirements for Fall 2027 Admission", columns General Test and Subject Test. Rows: Biophysics | "Optional/Not Required" | "Subject test in Physics or Biology - Optional/Not Required" ; Ecology and Evolutionary Biology | "Optional/Not Required" | "Subject test in Biology - Optional/Not Required" ; Molecular Biology | "Optional/Not Required" ; Neuroscience | "Optional/Not Required" ; Quantitative & Computational Biology | "Not Accepted".

### https://gradschool.princeton.edu/admission-onboarding/prepare/letters-recommendation
- "Three letters of recommendation are required. To ensure that each application receives a thorough and thoughtful review, we limit the number of letters of recommendation to three."
- "Letters must be written in English and come from faculty members or other individuals well acquainted with your academic work."

### https://gradschool.princeton.edu/admission-onboarding/prepare/academic-statement-purpose

- "The essay must be written in English and should not exceed 1,000 words."

### https://gradschool.princeton.edu/admission-onboarding/prepare/personal-statement
- "Applicants will be required to upload a personal statement with the admission application in the space provided."
- "The essay must be written in English and should not exceed 250 words."

### https://gradschool.princeton.edu/admission-onboarding/prepare/resume-curriculum-vitae
- "Applicants will be required to upload a resume or curriculum vitae with the admission application in the space provided."

### https://gradschool.princeton.edu/admission-onboarding/prepare/transcripts
- "As part of the admission application, applicants are required to upload unofficial transcripts, including the transcript key, for each institution they are currently attending or have previously attended."
- "For the purposes of the application process, there is no need to have your university send official paper or electronic transcripts to the Graduate Admission Office."

### https://gradschool.princeton.edu/admission-onboarding/prepare/fall-semester-grades
- "Applicants will be encouraged to upload a current transcript which includes the fall semester grades from the college or university where they are currently enrolled."
- "If an applicant is not a current student or if the school does not provide fall semester grades, fall semester grades are not required."

### https://gradschool.princeton.edu/admission-onboarding/apply
- "The application for Fall 2027 is now open. Use the link below to start an application:"
- Button "START YOUR FALL 2027 APPLICATION" links to https://graduate-apply.princeton.edu/apply/ ; "Application Checklist" links to the login page of the same site.
- "Specific degree application deadline dates can be found on our website under the Application Deadlines page."
- "We do not accept late applications, and our application process is fully electronic. There is no spring admission, and graduate study at Princeton is offered on a full-time basis."
- "You may apply to only one academic degree program for one degree per year."

### https://gradschool.princeton.edu/admission-onboarding/apply/application-tips
- "During the application process, you will be required to upload a statement of academic purpose, resume/curriculum vitae, transcripts, fall semester grades, and additional required files as specified by the individual degree program requirements."
- "International Students: There is only one application, which can be used by U.S. citizens, permanent residents or international applicants for any academic department or program."
- "Revisions: Applications and supporting materials are considered final upon submission and cannot be revised, replaced, or updated."

### https://gradschool.princeton.edu/admission-onboarding/apply/decision-notification
- "Admission decisions are emailed separately by department or program starting at the end of January through March 15. All decisions are sent to all applicants no later than March 15."
- "If placed on the waitlist, it might be closer to April 15 before receiving a final admission decision."
- "The Graduate School must receive a submitted acceptance by April 15, 11:59 p.m. ET or the offer of admission may be rescinded."

### https://gradschool.princeton.edu/admission-onboarding/explore
- "Our defined Ph.D. program length is generally five years, with a few exceptions for four-year programs."
- "For Ph.D. students, Princeton provides full financial support for all years of regular program enrollment, contingent on satisfactory academic performance."
- FAQ: "Is it necessary to contact a faculty member before I apply?" ; "No, it is not necessary to have a faculty member sponsor your application. You may contact faculty with questions, but it is not required."

### https://gradschool.princeton.edu/financial-support/financial-support-model
- "Princeton offers financial support for all Ph.D. students throughout their period of regular enrollment. This includes full tuition, university student health plan coverage, and a base stipend intended to cover estimated living expenses."
- "Support may include internal fellowships, teaching and research assistantships, and external fellowships, which together cover tuition, the student health plan, and the full base stipend for the duration of regular enrollment."

### https://gradschool.princeton.edu/financial-support/financial-support-model/phd-funding-natural-sciences-engineering
- "Princeton is committed to comprehensive financial support for Ph.D. students throughout regular program enrollment. This includes tuition, student health plan coverage, and stipend support from fellowships, assistantships, and external sources."
- "In the first year, doctoral students in the natural sciences and engineering receive full support through a 10‑month First‑Year Fellowship."
- "After the first academic year, departments support continuing students through Research Assistantships (RAs) or Teaching Assistantships (TAs) at standard University rates."
- "Summer research conducted with an adviser in June and July is paid at the same monthly RA rate as during the academic year."
