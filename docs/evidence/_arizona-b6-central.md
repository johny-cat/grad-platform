# Arizona B6 central policies

Checked 2026-10-30. Opened text: work/public-b6-arizona-more.json, -policy.json and -fees.json.

[International applicants](https://grad.arizona.edu/admissions/requirements/international-applicants), Acceptable Credentials and Exemptions: “minimum composite score of 7, with no subject area below a 6”; “The Graduate College does not accept Duolingo scores”. TOEFL before January 21, 2026: 79 iBT/Home Edition; on/after: 4 overall. IELTS/Online accepted, One Skill Retake excluded; PTE 60; CEPT Full Academic 110 or CESL endorsement; doctoral TOEFL Essentials excluded. Scores within two years of enrollment; departments may require more. Exempt English-official citizenship, completed accredited US bachelor/graduate degree or approved Guide degree. Unfinished degree: two full-time years with 48 upper-division undergraduate or 30 graduate graded credits at US/approved institutions, credits within two years. Teaching speaking standards are separate: legacy TOEFL 24, new 5. English-medium attendance alone does not qualify for teaching.

[Fees](https://grad.arizona.edu/admissions/procedures/application-fees), All Graduate Applicants: “Degree Seeking, $90.00”; “The Graduate College does not waive application fees.” Applies to all applicants; nonrefundable; separate application/fee per program. Department offers are separate.

[Entrance exams](https://grad.arizona.edu/admissions/requirements/entrance-examinations): “The University of Arizona Graduate College does not require entrance exams”; departments may require GRE. Program-specific GRE remains unknown where no explicit policy was found.
