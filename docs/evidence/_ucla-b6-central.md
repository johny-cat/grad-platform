# UCLA B6 central policies

Checked 2026-10-30. Opened text: work/public-b6-ucla-more.json and -policy.json.

[English](https://grad.ucla.edu/admissions/english-requirements/), Prior education, Exam score and ESLPE: “Minimum overall band score on IELTS Academic: 7.0.” TOEFL 87 before January 21, 2026 or 4.5 on/after. Home Edition and IELTS Online accepted; MyBest excluded; only most recent score considered; two-year validity. An accredited US bachelor/higher degree or WHED sole-English-instruction degree exempts. Non-English daily-life satellite campus requires recent test. Citizenship, residence and work do not substitute. Below TOEFL 100/5 or IELTS 7.5 requires ESLPE and possible ESL courses; TA oral assessment is separate. Previous Paper Edition or IELTS Indicator results remain valid two years.

[FAQ](https://grad.ucla.edu/admissions/faqs/), minimum TOEFL: “Writing – 25; Speaking – 24; Reading – 21; Listening – 17.” The central minimum degree is a comparable bachelor with B/3 average, or related graduate study average 3.

[Application policies](https://grad.ucla.edu/admissions/research-requirements/), fees: “$155.00: All other applicants”; US citizen/PR $135, nonrefundable. Waiver sections: listed program participation with verification; enrolled need-based aid (non-US/PR aid must come from California) with aid-officer letter, or registrar plus FAFSA; nonenrolled qualifying US income-tax return; designated recent 90-day benefits proof; in-person recruitment events for 2026–2027 application fees, subject to change. “Only attendees at in-person events are eligible to receive this fee waiver.” Conditional routes; international applicants must confirm program approval.
