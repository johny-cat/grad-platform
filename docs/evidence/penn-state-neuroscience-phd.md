# penn-state-neuroscience-phd

## Official details checked 2026-10-23

https://bulletins.psu.edu/graduate/programs/majors/neuroscience-science/
> “The application deadline is December 15 for admission in the following fall.”
> “Prospective applicants should have a bachelor's degree in a biological, physical, or behavioral science and are expected to have taken undergraduate courses in biology, chemistry, physics, and mathematics.”
> “Applicants are expected to have a 3.0 (B) grade point average or better.”
> “Neuroscience courses are desirable but not essential and research experience is an advantage.”
> “personal statement of purpose”; “official transcripts from all post-secondary institutions attended”; “three letters of recommendation”; “TOEFL/IELTS scores (if applicable).”
> “International students will typically be interviewed virtually.”
> “Qualified applicants generally will be offered a visit the UP campus for an interview.”
> “During the first year, students complete three research rotations”
> “These rotations serve to inform the students with regard to choosing an adviser and forming a committee.”
> “Graduate assistantships available to students in this program”
This is the Science/University Park program linked from Huck, not the separate Hershey Neuroscience program.
