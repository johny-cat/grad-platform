# University of Washington central pages (UW Graduate School, Seattle)
Checked 2026-10-20 by opening the pages in a headless browser (Chromium) and saving their full text; quotes are copied from that text. Program evidence files refer to these pages by URL. Collapsed sections of the Requirements and Fee Waivers pages were read from the page text without clicking (they hold the English test table and the McNair and stacked-degree waivers).

## Central facts (used by the UW programs checked here)
- application fee 90 USD (PhD programs), paid online: https://grad.uw.edu/prospective-students/how-to-apply/apply-now/ — "The nonrefundable application fee is $90.00 (USD) or $75.00 for Graduate Non-Matriculated applications." ; "Note: each submitted application requires a separate fee."
- fee waivers (domestic only in effect): https://grad.uw.edu/prospective-students/how-to-apply/application-fee-waivers/ — "Application fee waivers are available to U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and undocumented applicants who demonstrate financial need or hardship." ; "Applicants who expect to hold or currently hold F-1 or J-1 visa status are not eligible for this fee waiver due to federal eligibility requirements." ; McNair and PPIA: "Application fee waivers are available to U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and undocumented applicants who are current or former participants in approved graduate preparation programs" ; the stacked-degree waiver is for applicants to stacked degrees and their certificates only (not used by these PhD programs)
- waiver timing: same page — "Application fee waiver requests must be made at least seven (7) days before your program’s application deadline to allow time for review." ; "After submitting your application, do not pay the application fee if you have requested an application fee waiver." ; "Graduate Enrollment Management Services (GEMS) cannot waive or refund the application fee once it has been paid."
- deadlines are set per program, listed per entry term: https://grad.uw.edu/prospective-students/how-to-apply/apply-now/ — "Programs open for admission at different times and have varying deadlines." ; each program's detail page in the Graduate School's Find a Program list has a "Program Deadlines" block for "Autumn 2027" (extracts below)
- time of day for deadlines: https://grad.uw.edu/prospective-students/how-to-apply/apply-now/application-help-and-faqs/ — "If a department has a hard deadline, the application closes at 11:59 p.m. on the day of the deadline, in this example: you have until 11:59 p.m. on Dec. 1 to submit your application." (no time zone named; used only as background, never as a program's stated time)
- minimum GPA 3.0 for all programs: https://grad.uw.edu/prospective-students/how-to-apply/requirements/ — "Have at least a 3.0 cumulative grade-point-average on a 4 point scale"
- GRE is a program decision: application help page — "The Graduate School does not require the Graduate Record Exam (GRE). Graduate programs determine whether or not to require the GRE for the admissions review process."
- English proof is required of non-native speakers; minimum and recommended scores (table below); programs may ask for more: https://grad.uw.edu/prospective-students/how-to-apply/requirements/ — "Demonstration of English proficiency is required of all non-native English speakers." ; "Applicants who do not meet the minimum required score will not be considered for admission. We do not offer conditional admission." ; "Some graduate programs may require a higher score beyond the minimum required score."
- English exemption by degree taught in English, with residence rules and a list of countries: https://grad.uw.edu/policies/3-2-graduate-school-english-language-proficiency-requirements/ — "An earned and bachelor’s, master’s, or doctoral degree (or an internationally recognized academic degree at the same level) from an accredited institution for which English was the medium of instruction."
- test scores: valid two years, sent electronically, UW institution code 4854: Policy 3.2 — "Test scores are valid for two years and must be valid on the date the application is submitted or on the date the program requests a deferral petition" ; application help page — "Our institution code is: 4854."
- below the recommended score: Policy 3.2 — "An admitted applicant who scores below the Recommended level will be required to satisfactorily complete one or two UW Academic English Program (AEP) courses to meet the ELP requirement."
- international applicants accepted, same requirements: https://grad.uw.edu/prospective-students/how-to-apply/requirements/ — "The Graduate School accepts applications from U.S. citizens, permanent residents (green card holders), immigrants and international applicants." ; "Graduate School admission requirements and application procedures are the same for all applicants regardless of citizenship and visa status."
- salary of research assistants in biomedical programs, 2026-27: https://facstaff.grad.uw.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026-27_variable-RA-salary-schedule.pdf (linked from https://grad.uw.edu/graduate-student-funding/funding-information-for-departments/administering-assistantships/ta-ra-salaries) — row "Biomedical Research Departments*" shows $4,116 in every Sch. 1&3 column (extract below). Used only for the Neuroscience stipend, because that program's own page says its students are paid this schedule.
- applyUrl used in the program files: https://grad.uw.edu/prospective-students/how-to-apply/apply-now/ (the page with "Start Your Application"; it links to https://apply.grad.uw.edu/portal/gr_app)

## Differences between the Graduate School's own pages (the higher figure is used, per docs/DATA.md)
- Duolingo: the Requirements page and the International Applicants page give a minimum of 110 and a recommended score of 125 or higher; Policy 3.2 (revised September 2026) gives a minimum of 115 and a recommended score of 130 or higher. The program files use the higher figures (115 and 130) and say so in their notes.
- TOEFL MyBest: the Requirements page and International Applicants page list a "TOEFL MyBest" column (minimum 80, recommended 92 or higher); Policy 3.2 says "[Note: TOEFL MyBest and IELTS One Skill Retake tests scores are not admissible]". The program files do not mention MyBest.

### https://grad.uw.edu/policies/3-2-graduate-school-english-language-proficiency-requirements/
- "English language proficiency (ELP) is required for graduate study at the University of Washington. Therefore, every applicant whose native language is not English must demonstrate English language proficiency."
- Ways to show proficiency: "An earned and bachelor’s, master’s, or doctoral degree (or an internationally recognized academic degree at the same level) from an accredited institution for which English was the medium of instruction." ; residence: "For institutions located in an English-speaking country: The student must have been in residence in that country for the full duration of enrollment in the degree program" ; documentation is required "except for degrees from accredited institutions in the following countries: Anguilla, Antigua and Barbuda, Australia, the Bahamas, Barbados, Belize, Bermuda, Cayman Islands, Canada, Dominica, Ghana, Grenada, Guyana, Ireland, Jamaica, Kenya, Liberia, Montserrat, New Zealand, Nigeria, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Singapore, South Africa, Trinidad and Tobago, Turks and Caicos Islands, United Kingdom, the United States, or the Virgin Islands."
- "Official English Language Test Score (sent electronically to the University of Washington via testing center). An offer of admission cannot be extended prior to the receipt of official scores. Test scores are valid for two years and must be valid on the date the application is submitted or on the date the program requests a deferral petition"
- Table, columns "TOEFL iBT", "Duolingo", "Academic IELTS". Row "Minimum Required Score" ("ELP Not Satisfied; additional English language requirements as described in 3.2.2"): 4.0 (80 for tests taken before Jan 21, 2026) | 115 | 6.5. Row "Recommended Score" ("ELP Satisfied without conditions"): 4.5 (92 for tests taken before Jan 21, 2026) | 130 or higher | 7.0 or higher. Row "Test Format Allowed": Test Center or Home | At Home | Test Center or Home.
- "[Note: TOEFL MyBest and IELTS One Skill Retake tests scores are not admissible]"
- "A graduate program may set additional standards or qualifications in either of the above categories. Students must confirm specific requirements with the admitting program."
- "An applicant who has attained a qualifying degree or a Recommended Score on an official English language test per 3.2.1 has satisfied the Graduate School’s ELP requirement without conditions."
- "An admitted applicant who scores below the Recommended level will be required to satisfactorily complete one or two UW Academic English Program (AEP) courses to meet the ELP requirement."
