## Two-step application; General admission requirements; English; deadline table

Source: https://www.publichealth.indiana.edu/doctoral/apply/index.html

Exact anchor: “You must submit both applications to be considered for review.”

SOPHAS plus invited IU supplement are required. Biostatistics accepts bachelor’s or higher. Statement maximum 600 words addresses motivation, readiness and qualities; CV; most programs require three recommendations unless noted. All institutions’ transcripts required; official PhD records through SOPHAS. International applicants upload unofficial academic/degree records and certified translations; official verification may follow. Foreign PhD records require WES/ECE course-by-course evaluation directly to SOPHAS. Degree-conferral transcript within three months of completion. English: TOEFL 4, IELTS 6.5, DET 105. US/listed-English-country degree or listed citizenship may exempt; funding may still require testing. Biostatistics fall table gives Dec. 15 priority, Jan. 15 final, without year. GRE and fees not established.

## Doctoral costs and funding

Source: https://www.publichealth.indiana.edu/doctoral/cost.html

Exact anchor: “funding is not guaranteed”

Most doctoral students receive competitive academic appointments with full tuition, monthly stipend and health insurance. Funding is not guaranteed; automatic consideration, no separate funding application; apply by priority date.
