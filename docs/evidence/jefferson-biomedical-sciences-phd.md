# PhD Programs, Jefferson College of Life Sciences

Checked 2026-10-24 (page fetcher; quotes as returned, some shortened).

- Admission model and programs: https://www.jefferson.edu/academics/colleges-schools-institutes/life-sciences/degrees-programs/phd-programs.html — "students select laboratory rotations and more specialized advanced courses during their first year" ; "By the end of the first year, students choose their thesis advisor and matriculate into one of the below PhD programs". Six programs: Biochemistry, Structural & Molecular Biology; Cell Biology & Regenerative Medicine; Genetics, Genomics & Cancer Biology; Immunology & Microbial Pathogenesis; Molecular Physiology & Translational Medicine; Neuroscience.
- All below from https://www.jefferson.edu/academics/colleges-schools-institutes/life-sciences/degrees-programs/phd-programs/applying/faq.html
- Deadline: "The priority application deadline is December 1st." (no year). "on a space available basis" for late applications.
- Interviews/decisions: interviews "between late January and mid-March"; acceptance notices "between February and May."
- Fee: "The application fee is $60.00 and needs to be submitted by credit card (Visa or Mastercard only)". Waiver: request with supporting documents to the Sr. Associate Director of Admissions; "The request will be reviewed and a decision will be returned on whether the fee can be waived."
- GRE: "The GRE is optional, not required, for the PHD program in Biomedical Sciences."
- English: official scores from "an accepted English proficiency exam such as TOEFL or IELTS." (no minimum)
- Letters: three required ("Applicants submit their letters in the Program section under Evaluations.").
- Documents: "The personal statement should be approximately 500-750 words", resume/CV, "Official transcripts from all institutions attended.", "submit a course-by-course evaluation from World Education Services (WES)" for non-US degrees.
- Funding: "Students accepted to a PhD program in JCLS receive a fellowship, which includes tuition, fees." ; "health insurance and a competitive stipend" (no amount). The same page quotes data for the 2018 cohort; not used.
- City: not stated on pages read.
