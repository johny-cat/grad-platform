## Fee and waiver
https://gsas.yale.edu/admissions/phdmasters-application-process/application-fees-fee-waivers

"The application fee for all degree-seeking applicants is US $105.00."
"US citizens, permanent residents, and non-US citizens are all eligible to request a fee waiver."
The page lists special programs/status and documented financial hardship as bases. Separate requests take "2-3 business days" and must be well before the program deadline. The JSON note paraphrases these restrictions.

Independent review reopened the page and simplified the fee note. Exact support: “fee waiver requests are submitted separately from the application for admission and usually take 2-3 business days to process.” “Please plan to submit your fee waiver request well in advance of your application deadline whenever possible.” The listed bases are “participation in a special program or status (see below)” and “documented financial hardship”.
