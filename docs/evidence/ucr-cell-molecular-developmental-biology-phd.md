## Requirements / Admissions

Last two undergraduate years GPA3.2; masters holders GPA3.0, master’s not required. Preparation: chemistry2years, physics/calculus1year each, statistics/biochemistry1course each, biology2years including genetics and2cell/molecular/developmental courses. Strong applicants can make up deficiencies in first2years. Optional GRE combined300 criterion. Admission model not established.

GRE optional. PhDdeadline December 1 undated.

## Funding

PhD students normally receive five years support; first-year fellowship/program GSR, later faculty grants/GSR/TA. Normally is not an unconditional all-admitted promise. Two quarters teaching required.
