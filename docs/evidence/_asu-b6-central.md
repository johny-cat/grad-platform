# ASU School of Life Sciences B6 shared evidence
Checked 2026-10-29. Source caches outside repository: work/public-b6-asu-*.json. Short verbatim anchors plus exact section references.

[Graduate FAQ](https://sols.asu.edu/admission/graduate/faq): adviser-contact question says not required, highly recommended to discover recruiting faculty; GRE question says scores not required for all SOLS programs, but applicants may send scores (4007). Bachelor directly to PhD allowed, other undergraduate fields reviewed, below-3 GPA holistically considered. Fee question: “a faculty member may pay for the application fee on your behalf.” Contact research-aligned potential adviser first; SOLS office cannot waive it. Funding question: “PhD students are guaranteed five years of funding”; assistantship stipend, tuition and health insurance; additional student/program fees remain student responsibility. English question requires full TA certification scores for TA appointments.

[Central admission](https://admission.asu.edu/apply/graduate/admission), Pay the application fee: “$120: International students on any nonimmigrant visa type studying in the U.S.” Domestic categories, permanent-residency applicants, DACA and international online students outside US $75; nonrefundable. Current central fee supersedes FAQ's older $70 figure.

[Central English](https://admission.asu.edu/apply/international/graduate/english-proficiency), tests and exemptions: “Overall band score on the academic test of at least 6.5.” TOEFL before January 21, 2026: 80; on/after: 4. DET 105, PTE 60. IELTS Academic/Online/UKVI/Indicator accepted; General Training excluded; PTE Academic Online excluded. TOEFL test-center accepted, Home Edition on/after January 21, 2026 accepted, Paper Edition excluded. Scores within two years of start. Programs can impose higher standards. US citizen/PR or listed-country citizen automatically exempt. After applying and waiting 72 hours, form routes include English-only bachelor/higher with institution letter or website; accredited US degree; recognized listed-country degree; nine qualifying graduate credits at GPA 3; 90 US undergraduate credits at GPA 3; or four years US high school with English courses.

[MCB program](https://sols.asu.edu/node/9851), Funding (also on ELS/Evolution pages): “will typically receive a funding offer as well.” Typical TA/RA each semester for five years, summer optional; biweekly stipend, tuition remission for 6–18 fall/spring and 1–14 summer credits, and health insurance. This qualified statement differs from the FAQ guarantee; the records disclose the difference.
