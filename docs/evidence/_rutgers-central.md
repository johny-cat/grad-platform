

## Official details reviewed 2026-10-23

Source: https://gradstudy.rutgers.edu/apply/application-guidelines (opened 2026-10-23)

> $70 nonrefundable application fee

> One official transcript from each post-secondary institution attended

> If you are a McNair, Project 1000, GEM, or other scholar, you may be eligible for an application fee waiver.

> Applicants who are currently serving in the U.S. Military or are Military Veterans who plan to apply for educational benefits under the GI Bill, receive a waiver of the application fee.

Source: https://gradstudy.rutgers.edu/information/international-students (opened 2026-10-23)

> Score reports must be from tests taken within two years from the start of the semester to which you are applying and sent directly to us from the testing agency.

> TOEFL taken through January 21, 2026:

> Writing 22, Speaking 23, Reading 21, and Listening 17

> TOEFL taken January 22, 2026 onward:

> Writing 4.5, Speaking 4.5, Reading 4, and Listening 4

> Minimum overall IELTS score for admission consideration is a bandwidth 7

> Minimum DET score for admission consideration ranges between 120-130

> The English proficiency test requirement may be waived for applicants who have completed their entire degree at an institution where English is the principal language of instruction.

> Requests for waivers are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.
