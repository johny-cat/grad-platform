## Admissions process; Finding an advisor; Writing your personal statement

Source: https://bio.indiana.edu/graduate/genome-cell-development/steps.html

Exact anchor: “nearly all students admitted to the GCDB graduate program rotate”

Committee review considers preparation, research experience, communication and faculty fit. Nearly all students rotate for three five-week labs in the first semester; a direct-admit exception is reserved for strong relevant research experience with select faculty. Faculty contact is recommended. Statements discuss scientific interests, development, research, goals and faculty. Overseas interviews are by video; US-based invitees receive recruitment travel/lodging. Offers usually begin in March. All admitted students receive five full funding years, fellowship/TA/RA support, health insurance and free tuition.

## Entrance requirements; Application materials; English; Application Cost

Source: https://bio.indiana.edu/graduate/how-to-apply/phd-apply.html

Exact anchor: “GRE Scores are Not Required”

Biology/related major and laboratory background; exceptions possible. Approximately 500-word statement, CV, all UG/graduate transcripts, recommender details (count unstated) and English scores. International applicants upload unofficial records; official documents requested on admission. Recommended legacy TOEFL 79, IELTS 6.5, DET 115; two years from application. Official scores complete the application. Official-English-country citizenship or US/UK/Canada degree exempts; other waiver requests to department. Fee $70 for all; no department waiver/reimbursement; central exceptions linked.
