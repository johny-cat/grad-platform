# Harvard central pages (Harvard Kenneth C. Griffin Graduate School of Arts and Sciences, "Griffin GSAS")
Checked 2026-10-20 with WebFetch (verbatim quotes as returned; WebFetch limits each quote to 125 characters, so long sentences appear in pieces joined by " ... "). The program evidence files refer to these pages by URL.

## Central facts (used by all seven Harvard programs)
- deadline time rule (5:00 p.m. Eastern): https://gsas.harvard.edu/apply — "All components of the application to a degree program are due by 5:00 p.m., Eastern Time, on the deadline date."
- application fee 105 USD: https://gsas.harvard.edu/apply/applying-degree-programs/completing-your-application — "The application fee is $105 payable by credit card."
- fee waiver available (no domestic-only limit stated): https://gsas.harvard.edu/apply/applying-degree-programs/completing-your-application — "Applicants can determine eligibility for a fee waiver by completing a series of questions" ... "in the Application Fee section of the application." ; "Once these questions have been answered, the application system will provide an immediate response regarding fee waiver eligibility." ; "The application fee is not refundable."
- letters of recommendation, 3: https://gsas.harvard.edu/apply/applying-degree-programs/letters-recommendation — "Harvard Griffin GSAS requires three letters of recommendation."
- unofficial transcripts: https://gsas.harvard.edu/apply/applying-degree-programs/transcripts — "Applicants are required to upload unofficial transcripts from each institution attended."
- English minimums (TOEFL new scale 5 / Speaking 4.5, earlier tests 95 / 23; IELTS 7 / Speaking 6.5; exemption; two-year validity): https://gsas.harvard.edu/apply/applying-degree-programs/english-proficiency
- international applicants welcome: https://gsas.harvard.edu/apply — "Immigration status does not factor into decisions about admissions and financial aid." ; https://gsas.harvard.edu/apply/applying-degree-programs — "Visa documentation is not required at the time of application."
- years of funding by program (six years for MCB, OEB, Biophysics, SSQB, Neuroscience, Virology, Biomedical Informatics): https://gsas.harvard.edu/natural-sciences-engineering-and-applied-sciences-and-medical-sciences-support
- applyUrl: https://apply.gsas.harvard.edu/portal/apply_degree — the "Apply to Degree Programs" link on https://gsas.harvard.edu/apply; the page opened and is titled "Degree Application Management" ("Applicants to Degree Programs", with "Log in here" and "Create an account").

"Deadlines vary by graduate program and are noted on graduate program pages."
"The application fee is $105" payable by credit card.

"Applications are reviewed by the admissions committee based on the program you applied to."
"welcomes applications for admission from individuals who have or will have by the time of matriculation a BA, BS, or equivalent undergraduate degree" ... "(for prospective international students, a three- or four-year undergraduate degree" ... "from an institution of recognized standing)."

"The application fee is $105 payable by credit card."

### https://gsas.harvard.edu/apply/applying-degree-programs/english-proficiency
(Same text also opened at https://gsas.harvard.edu/apply/applying-degree-programs/completing-your-application/english-proficiency)
"Adequate command of spoken and written English is essential to success in graduate study at Harvard."
"Applicants who are non-native English speakers can demonstrate English proficiency in one of three ways:"
"Receiving an undergraduate degree from an academic institution where English is the primary language of instruction."
"Earning a minimum score of 5 (95 for tests taken prior to January 21, 2026) on the TOEFL iBT" ... "and a minimum Speaking score of 4.5 (23 for tests taken prior to January 21, 2026)."
"Earning a minimum score of 7 on the IELTS Academic test and a minimum Speaking score of 6.5."
"A master's degree or other graduate degree is not accepted as proof of English proficiency."
"Some degree programs may require higher scores on either the TOEFL or IELTS."
"Alternate language tests, certifications, or individual examples of English language experience are not accepted"
"TOEFL/IELTS scores are valid for no longer than two years."
"If you are applying for admission for fall 2027, you may submit scores from tests taken no earlier than January 5, 2025."
"Use ETS code 3451 to send TOEFL scores to Harvard Griffin GSAS."
NOTE: this page says 95 for TOEFL tests before 21 January 2026; the GSAS program pages for Virology, Neuroscience, Molecular and Cellular Biology and Biomedical Informatics say 100 (see their evidence files). The Biophysics requirements page repeats 95.

### https://gsas.harvard.edu/apply/applying-degree-programs/letters-recommendation
"Harvard Griffin GSAS requires three letters of recommendation."
"You may ask additional recommenders to submit letters if you choose" ... "it is not guaranteed that the admissions committee will review additional letters."
"At least one letter should be from a faculty member at the school where you earned your most recent degree"
"Yes. Please remember that letters of recommendation must be received by the graduate program’s application deadline."
"Harvard Griffin GSAS does not accept letters submitted via email, mail, or a dossier service."

### https://gsas.harvard.edu/apply/applying-degree-programs/transcripts
"Applicants are required to upload unofficial transcripts from each institution attended."
"Harvard Griffin GSAS does not accept transcripts received by mail or email"
"Official transcripts are only required from those who have been admitted and accepted the offer of admission."
"Transcripts from international institutions must be in English or be accompanied by a certified English translation."
