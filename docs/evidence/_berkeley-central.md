- Application fee: "The non-refundable application fee is $135 for U.S. citizens and current permanent residents" ; "and $155 for all other applicants." ; "A 4.22% payment processing fee will be added to all application fee payments."
- Fee waiver: "U.S. citizens or current permanent residents who demonstrate financial need or have participated in an eligible program" ; "may apply for an application fee waiver."
- "Some programs do not require the application fee payment."

- "We do not accept the Duolingo exam, TOEFL ITP, My Best Scores, IELTS Indicator, or IELTS One Test Retake."
- TOEFL: "We accept the internet-based (iBT) and iBT Home Edition. We do not accept the TOEFL ITP or My Best Scores" ; "Tests taken from June 1, 2025 to January 20, 2026: 90 minimum total score" ; "Tests taken on or after January 21, 2026: 5 minimum total score, with 4.5 minimum Speaking and Writing section scores." ; "For Fall 2027, tests taken before June 1, 2025 will not be accepted, even if your score was reported to UC Berkeley."
- IELTS: "We accept the IELTS and IELTS Online. We do not accept the IELTS Indicator or One Test Retake" ; "Tests taken from June 1, 2025 to January 20, 2026: 7 minimum overall band score" ; "Tests taken on or after January 21, 2026: 7 overall band score, with 6.5 minimum Speaking and Writing section scores" ; "For Fall 2027, tests taken before June 1, 2025 will not be accepted."
- Exemption: "To qualify for a TOEFL/IELTS exemption, you must fulfill one of the following options:"
  - Option 1: "Have a basic degree from a recognized institution in a country where the primary language" "used in both daily life and academic instruction is English."
  - Option 2: "Have completed a basic or advanced degree at an institution in the United States or a United States institution abroad," "where the language of instruction is English, and the institution is accredited by one of the United States' regional" "accrediting agencies."
  - Option 3: "Have completed at least one year of full-time academic course work with grades of B or better" "at a regionally accredited institution within the United States."
  - "The TOEFL or IELTS must be submitted by applicants who do not meet the exemption criteria above."
- Letters: "The application requires letters of recommendation." (no number given on this page)
- Documents: "Upload unofficial transcripts (ie, a scanned copy) from every post-secondary school that you have attended" ; "Upload a scanned copy of your transcript and degree certificate for each institution after high school." ; "Submit a Statement of Purpose and Personal History statement, along with any other essays the program you are applying to requires."
