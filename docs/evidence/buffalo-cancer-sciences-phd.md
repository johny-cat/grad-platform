## Identity, application and tracks

> Doctoral training at Roswell Park Comprehensive Cancer Center is offered through an academic collaboration with the University at Buffalo (UB).

> Applications for Fall 2027 enrollment in the PhD in Cancer Sciences program will be accepted from September 1, 2026 through December 5, 2026.

Table text: “Program Start Date”; “Fall Semester: August 23, 2027”.

> After your first semester, you’ll choose your path in of our five PhD tracks:

Linked list: “Cancer Prevention PhD Track”; “Cancer Genomics and Computational Oncology PhD Track”; “Tumor Immunology PhD Track”; “Cell Stress and Biophysical Oncology PhD Track”; “Experimental Therapeutics PhD Track”.

> You’ll select your research track at the end of your first semester, then begin three laboratory research rotations in your second semester to find the best match for your faculty mentors.

FAQ asks whether one can apply directly to a mentor's laboratory:

> No. Instead, you must apply through the normal process. If invited for an interview you will have the opportunity to request to interview with the mentor in question. If accepted into the program, you will have three rotations with faculty mentors prior to selecting the mentor with whom you will do your dissertation work.

## Requirements

Eligibility bullets:

> A bachelor's degree, or the equivalent, from an accredited/government recognized college or university. Most such degrees will be four or more years in length; UB recognizes some three-year degrees, but not all. The University at Buffalo does accept all three-year degrees from accredited Indian institutions.

> A B-grade (GPA of 3.0) or higher from your bachelor's degree (or equivalent) courses. Grade point averages from non-U.S. universities will be evaluated by UB's Office of International Admissions

> $75 application fee
> Unofficial Undergraduate transcripts from every college and university attended – must have a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 (official transcripts are required once accepted in the program).
> Personal statement (no word limit). Be sure to highlight your successful completion of biological coursework and previous research!
> Three letters of recommendation
> Current resume or CV

> Unofficial WES evaluation report required for all international transcripts

> Students who are already enrolled in a PhD program elsewhere may apply to transfer to Roswell Park’s Graduate Program. Transfer applicants must follow the regular application procedure. At least one of your letters must come from a faculty member at your current institution who is familiar with your work, and your personal statement should include your reasons for wanting to transfer programs.

## Funding, international applicants and waivers

> All doctoral students are offered a stipend, a full tuition scholarship and paid student fees.

> Importantly, this support is guaranteed for the duration of training. We also offer additional financial incentives for students who are awarded extramural fellowships.

> No. Admitted students receive full tuition and a stipend. Tuition and stipend are awarded throughout the training period, contingent upon students remaining in good academic standing.
